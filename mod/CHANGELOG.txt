Starsector Marines - Changelog
==============================

0.1.2-alpha
-----------
Interface
  - The company screens take the base game's look: its blue panels, buttons
    and type sizes, the sky of wherever your fleet is behind every page, and
    a page bar in the bottom left whose routes take the letter key
    underlined in their names, as the game's own bar does.
  - Missions is a route on the page bar and greys out when nothing is on
    offer. New things on any page are marked with a gold symbol.
  - A Settings page, starting with a Voice-over switch that silences the
    mod's recorded speech.
  - Out-of-combat screens keep their controls at 125%, 133% and 150% UI
    scale.
  - The briefing is trimmed to what matters: mission name, scale, risk and
    reward (with the credit sign), and warning lamps for strength and lift.
  - Barracks reads people first: squad musters drawn with the battle's own
    sprites, lost marines' seats left empty where they stood. The Company
    Roster is gone.
  - The debrief shows your field force as the battle left it and how long
    until it is fully repaired.
  - Castle's tutorial is reworded and shortened, in one callout style.

Company
  - The first squad and first shuttle are free; the prepaid stores are gone.
  - The First Refit is one choice that fits every line squad at once.
  - Casualties are a dice roll: a downed marine comes home fit, with the
    wound on their record, or is killed. Nobody waits in a wounded list.
  - Mechs, vehicles and shuttles share one kind of stores (spare parts) and
    one way to fit, strip, buy and recycle. A bought machine stands with
    nothing fitted, plates can be lifted off to stores, and parts keep
    their places.
  - Engineering shows the company's machines as fitted, and the HQ command
    deck is rebuilt as the ship's bridge.

Machines
  - The Hegemony armour line: the Onager heavy repulsor tank, the Scorpion
    medium tank, and the APC with its mortar and LRM carriers, in Hegemony,
    Independent and pirate paint. The dozer breacher is retired.
  - Weight decides shoving, crushing and ramming. Heavy hulls crush across
    their whole footprint and break ordinary walls, props and furniture.
  - Large pulse lasers (the PL-3, and the PL-3 (Pulse), formerly Sweep) pass
    through one soldier, splash, wear walls and can light grass. The AC-6
    and AC-6 (QS) are retuned as medium and cheap calibres.
  - Drone bays rebuild every drone they lose, and the FL-THRM's tank refills.
  - Debug Missions has a Proving Ground with every mech and vehicle parked
    in ranks to drive.

Battles
  - Vehicles no longer pile up on each other or stand stuck for long, and
    battles run smoother.
  - Infantry stall far less, stop flickering between facings, step out of
    fire, and run through it when ordered to.
  - Trees stop rounds only at the trunk and dense core.
  - Squad radio chatter and Castle's replies in battle are gone; story lines
    remain. The battle HUD uses the base game's combat colours.
  - Wolf Strike, Onslaught Drop and the Mudskipper are fleet support, called
    with the committed ship.
  - A Cold Account ends when the company rallies at the pickup.
  - Depot Clearance meets a full force, and the Dust Ledger plays its own
    briefing.

Fixes
  - Closing the company dialog no longer leaves the campaign map's
    background white.
  - A missing catalog manifest no longer stops launch under a mod that
    replaces the file loader.
  - A replayed company-bay chapter grants its armour drop again.

0.1.1-alpha (hotfix)
--------------------
  - Fixed: the game no longer fails to launch with Starsector Marines and
    other mods enabled together (a SecurityException on startup).
  - Fixed: with Nexerelin or another mod that ships its own weapon or hull
    tables enabled, warships over a battle (the Onslaught, your fleet's
    support ships) came up with no guns.
  - Weapons from other mods fitted to your support ships now fire as the
    closest base-game weapon of the same mount, size and role, so a modded
    fit still arms the ship.

0.1.0-alpha (first public alpha)
--------------------------------
First public build.

  - Marine company: a captain, named officers, squads and fire teams, a
    reserve, equipment issue from the Fleet Armory, recovery and replacement
    of losses.
  - Ground operations: real-time tactical battles inside the planet dialog,
    on generated colony maps, with fog of war, cover, buildings, vehicles,
    mechs, aircraft and orbital support.
  - Conquest as the main mode, toned down for early progression, with
    opening story work from Zoey Venn on Jangala and repeatable work from
    other Independent brokers afterwards.
  - Mech bay: persistent lances of mechs and vehicles with refits.
  - Optional Nexerelin integration.

Known issues
  - Alpha balance: force ratios, rewards and difficulty ramps are still being
    tuned; some operations are much harder or easier than intended.
  - Saves are not guaranteed to carry over between alpha versions.
  - Adding the mod to an existing campaign is not supported.
  - Large maps can be slow on older CPUs, especially in Conquest.
  - Some text is not yet localised and some screens still use placeholder
    copy.
