Starsector Marines - Changelog
==============================

0.1.0-alpha (first public alpha)
--------------------------------
First public build.

  - Marine company: a captain, named officers, squads and fire teams, a
    reserve, equipment issue from the Fleet Armory, recovery and replacement
    of losses.
  - Ground operations: real-time tactical battles inside the planet dialog,
    on generated colony maps, with fog of war, cover, buildings, vehicles,
    mechs, aircraft and orbital support.
  - Conquest as the main mode, toned down for early progression, with
    opening story work from Zoey Venn on Jangala and repeatable work from
    other Independent brokers afterwards.
  - Mech bay: persistent lances of mechs and vehicles with refits.
  - Optional Nexerelin integration.

Known issues
  - Alpha balance: force ratios, rewards and difficulty ramps are still being
    tuned; some operations are much harder or easier than intended.
  - Saves are not guaranteed to carry over between alpha versions.
  - Adding the mod to an existing campaign is not supported.
  - Large maps can be slow on older CPUs, especially in Conquest.
  - Some text is not yet localised and some screens still use placeholder
    copy.
