Sheet: the research site's telling object, a sealed laboratory annex, 5x4 cells, five states of ONE object, left to right then the next row: (1) clean, (2) grown, (3) swallowed, (4) damaged, (5) destroyed.
(1) CLEAN: a squat windowless concrete annex from above: thick parapet walls, a pale flat roof inset, three round sealed steel roof vents, a dark roof plant strip, a heavy yellow-and-black airlock door on the south wall, hazard-yellow blocks along the north parapet. Secure, institutional, built to keep something in.
(2) GROWN: the same annex BLOWN OUTWARD FROM INSIDE: the roof gone, the walls thrown outward as tilted slabs lying away from the building on every side, concrete chunks scattered out, the clean white lab floor open to the sky with three burst round aquarium tanks along the north: steel-ringed, their glass broken outward from inside, drained, a last film of dark teal water in the bottom. Thick old creep rises from the floor and spreads out over the thrown walls. It must read, wordlessly, as "what they kept in here broke out".
(3) SWALLOWED: the same blown annex with the growth thickest: its west half buried under a raised creep mound with plates and spurs, the east wall slabs and white floor still showing.
(4) DAMAGED: state (2) after a fight: scorch, cracks, burnt and torn creep.
(5) DESTROYED: low separated concrete, steel and white floor fragments scattered wide, a thin dark creep scab at the bottom, transparent between pieces, no slab beneath.
