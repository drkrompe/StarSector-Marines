Sheet: the park's pond, where the first mother can open, 4x3 cells, five states of ONE object, left to right then the next row: (1) clean, (2) grown, (3) swallowed, (4) damaged, (5) destroyed.
(1) CLEAN: an ornamental park pond from above: a pale cut-stone rim round an oval of clear dark teal water, three green lily pads. Quiet, municipal, pretty.
(2) GROWN: the same pond DRAINED: the stone rim intact, the bed bare brown mud, and in the middle the bed torn open into a black hole from below; thick old creep wells up out of it and spills over the rim to the east and south. It must read, wordlessly, as "it came up through the pond".
(3) SWALLOWED: the drained, torn pond with the growth thickest: its west half drowned under a raised creep mound with plates and spurs, the east rim stones still showing.
(4) DAMAGED: state (2) after a fight: scorch, cracked rim stones, creep torn and burnt.
(5) DESTROYED: the dark muddy hollow and black hole left, the rim's cut stones thrown low round it, transparent between pieces outside the hollow.
