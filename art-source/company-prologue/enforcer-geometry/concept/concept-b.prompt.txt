Reference 1 is the game's top-down sprite: it is the authority for the hull's PLAN only (bow at the top of the sprite). Keep that plan recognisable from above: the nearly square armoured body; the five ring-mounted twin gun turrets (one on the centreline forward, two on the bow shoulders, two on the aft quarters); the four missile pods jutting forward; the broad anvil-shaped ram prow on a narrow neck; the two dark diamond-shaped wells either side of the waist; the two pale tank bays aft either side of a central spine with an octagonal bridge; the two engine blocks either side of a notched stern.

What the design must solve: the top surface must NOT be one flat deck with guns sitting on it. Give the ship a strong, interesting vertical silhouette, the way a real warship is built: a raised armoured citadel and command tower on the centreline that is the highest point; turrets on different tiers so they can fire over each other (the bow-shoulder turrets superfiring over the forward centreline turret); armour that falls away from the citadel in steep sloped glacis faces and stepped terraces; tall drive towers at the stern; the ram prow rising into a high brow. It should read as a ship from the side, with a clear profile, not as a slab or a table. Low-tech, heavily armoured, old, industrial: thick plate, seams, bolted panels, exposed structure, sloped armour. Pale warm-grey armour with slate-blue trim and dark gunmetal machinery.
