The material: the facade of a two-storey port office and arrivals building, 12 m wide and 10 m tall. Pale weathered precast concrete panels with dark joints, two long horizontal bands of dark tinted glass ribbon windows (one per storey, each about 1.6 m tall, with thin grey aluminium mullions every 1.5 m), a thin parapet capping along the top edge, dark rain streaks running down from the window sills and from the parapet, green-black algae staining low down and a dirt splash band along the bottom edge (the ground line). One external air-conditioning unit on a bracket under a window. Utilitarian government building, slightly run down.
