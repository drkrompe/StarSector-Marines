Flat, even, overcast daylight with no cast shadows and no strong directional shading, so it works as a colour (albedo) map that the scene lights itself. The LEFT and RIGHT edges must tile seamlessly: whatever crosses the left edge continues exactly at the right edge, with no seam, no vignette and no change of brightness across the image.
