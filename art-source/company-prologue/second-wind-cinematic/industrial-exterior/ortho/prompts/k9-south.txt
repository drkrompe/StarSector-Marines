This tile: 144 m wide by 96 m tall, so one pixel is about 9.4 cm. It is the south half of Kilo Nine, a private ship repair yard run by one tough salvage engineer, fenced, at the edge of the port.

What the regions are:
- Brown-grey ground everywhere between things: the yard's surface of compacted gravel and dirt. Tyre ruts, oil and fuel stains, damp patches and shallow puddles, scattered grit, a few weeds and grass tufts where nothing drives.

- The large pale grey disc at the right: the landing pad, a low concrete slab 96 m across. Cast concrete panels with joint lines, heavy scorching and soot fanning out from its centre where ships' drives fire on landing, oil rings, patch repairs.
