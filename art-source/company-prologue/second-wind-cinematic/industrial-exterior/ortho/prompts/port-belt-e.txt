This tile: 780 m wide by 520 m tall, so one pixel is about 51 cm. It is the east end of the industrial belt between the farmland (south, at the bottom) and the spaceport (north, at the top): a ditch and fence, a freight road, rail sidings, two rows of warehouses, the east end of the port's container yard and open ground running out to the jungle. Paint it like a real high-altitude aerial survey photograph of a hard-worked, rain-washed industrial district.

What the regions are, from the bottom up:
- The blue-grey line near the bottom edge: a drainage ditch of dark, still, muddy water with reedy banks. The olive band above it: a rough grassy verge with weeds, crossed by the two very thin straight lines of the belt's perimeter fence (a chain-link fence on posts, seen from above as a thin dark line; keep it thin).
- The wide dark grey band running left to right: the freight road, worn grey-black asphalt with patched cracks, tyre polish along its lanes and oil stains. No painted road lines.
- The three thin darker lines above it: rail sidings, each a bed of dark grey ballast stones with two thin steel rails on closely spaced sleepers.
- The flat brown-grey ground everywhere between things: compacted dirt and gravel yard surface, tyre tracks, oil stains, puddles, a few weeds at the edges. The large empty brown-grey area at the right: open, little-used yard ground, rougher and weedier, with old tyre tracks and puddles.
- The dark grey band running top to bottom at the far left: a cross street of worn asphalt, patched and stained. The cross streets run on through the container yard to the loading strip: keep them clear asphalt there, with no containers on them.
- The large rectangles in rust-orange, grey, off-white and green: warehouse roofs seen from above, corrugated or standing-seam metal in faded paint of that colour, with rust streaks, patched sheets and grime along the gutters. The long white stripes on some roofs: skylight strips of translucent ribbed panels. The small grey squares on the roofs: roof plant, air handlers and vent boxes. The slightly larger grey square inside the large off-white roof in the lower row: the top of a tall concrete chimney stack, its rim soot-blackened.
- The groups of two or three short white bars just below the lower row of warehouses: the white roofs of cargo trailers parked against the loading docks. Paint them only as those plain flat white rectangles.
- The pale grey area at the top left: the east end of the container yard, a stained concrete slab of large cast panels. The many small coloured bars on it: shipping containers seen from above, ribbed steel roofs in their own colours (rust red, blue, green, mustard yellow, grey, plum), scuffed and rust-edged, lined up in rows exactly as drawn. The yellow H-shape: a rail-mounted gantry crane, yellow-painted steel girders seen from directly above. Keep it exactly as drawn.
- The thin dark band along the top edge at the left: the asphalt loading strip at the edge of the port.
- The dark green strip along the very top edge at the right: the start of the jungle, dense wet undergrowth and ferns; the small brown patch in it: bare red-brown earth.
