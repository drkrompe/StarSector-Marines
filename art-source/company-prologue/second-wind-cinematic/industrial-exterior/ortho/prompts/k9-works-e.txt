This tile: 144 m wide by 96 m tall, so one pixel is about 9.4 cm. It is part of Kilo Nine, a private ship repair yard run by one tough salvage engineer, fenced, at the edge of the port, with the jungle beyond its east fence. This is the east side of its north half: the plate stock under the overhead crane, and the east fence.
