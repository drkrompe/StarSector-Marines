Grounded, photographic science-fiction film. A sheet of eight vehicle cards for compositing: heavy freight road trains of the Wayline drivers' cooperative on a tropical colony world, seen side-on from the left side and a little from above (about 13 degrees down), so each shows its whole side face and a thin sliver of its roof. Every vehicle faces RIGHT (cab at the right end).

Image 1 is the SILHOUETTE AND LAYOUT AUTHORITY: eight flat grey silhouettes on flat chroma green, two columns by four rows. Fill each silhouette exactly: same position, length, height, cab shape, wheel positions and view angle. Do not move, resize, rotate or add vehicles.
Image 2 is the IDENTITY reference: the game's own freight hauler, seen from directly above (a top-down sprite, not a pose). Background: keep the flat chroma green exactly, plain and uniform, everywhere outside the vehicles, including between stanchions and under the chassis. No ground, no road, no cast shadow on the green, no dust, no text, no logos, no labels.
Style: photographic realism, like a telephoto photograph of real vehicles. Not a toy, model, cartoon or painting.
