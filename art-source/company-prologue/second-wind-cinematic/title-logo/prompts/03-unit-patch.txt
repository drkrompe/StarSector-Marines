Use case: title logo for the end of a game cinematic. Direction: unit patch with a clean wordmark. Starsector Marines is a grounded game about a small mercenary marine company of refugees from a failing mining station, landing on a jungle world. The mark has two parts side by side: on the left, a round embroidered cloth unit patch with a merrowed edge, showing a simple emblem: a small boxy drop-ship silhouette descending toward the curve of a planet's horizon, three small stars above, in olive drab, sand, dark navy and a single small accent of muted orange, real embroidery stitch texture. On the right, a clean modern wordmark: MARINES in a bold, slightly extended geometric sans-serif in off-white, and STARSECTOR above it in small, wide-tracked light capitals, with a thin rule between them. The patch is about the height of both lines of text together. Calm, confident, hopeful, like a real service insignia. No glow, no chrome.

Lettering must be spelled exactly, in capital letters: the setting name STARSECTOR, small, and the mod's name MARINES, large and dominant; MARINES is the identity of the mark and STARSECTOR is a modest subtitle. No other words, numbers or letters anywhere. This is an original logo for a fan-made game mod: do not imitate the logo, typeface or emblem of any existing game, studio or franchise, and do not add trademark symbols. True transparent background, including the spaces inside and between letters; no ground, no backdrop, no drop shadow slab, no border, no frame, no mockup, no watermark. Wide landscape canvas. The whole mark centred with generous empty margin around it. Flat-on front view, not in perspective, not on an object. It will be composited over a bright aerial photograph of grey concrete and green jungle, so it needs strong value contrast at its edges and must stay legible at a quarter of screen width.
