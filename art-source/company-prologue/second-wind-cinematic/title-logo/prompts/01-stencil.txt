Use case: title logo for the end of a game cinematic. Direction: military stencil. Starsector Marines is a grounded game about a small mercenary marine company of refugees from a failing mining station, landing on a jungle world. The mark is a spray-painted military stencil, as if sprayed through a cut card onto a crate: heavy condensed stencil capitals with the characteristic bridges breaking each letter, slightly uneven overspray halo, a few drips and missed edges, slightly worn. MARINES in off-white bone paint with a thin dark charcoal outer edge for contrast; STARSECTOR above it in smaller widely spaced stencil capitals, centred, with a short stencilled bar on each side of it. Restrained palette: bone white and charcoal only. Hopeful but humble, functional, hand-made, not glossy. No metallic shine, no gradients, no 3D bevel.

Lettering must be spelled exactly, in capital letters: the setting name STARSECTOR, small, and the mod's name MARINES, large and dominant; MARINES is the identity of the mark and STARSECTOR is a modest subtitle. No other words, numbers or letters anywhere. This is an original logo for a fan-made game mod: do not imitate the logo, typeface or emblem of any existing game, studio or franchise, and do not add trademark symbols. True transparent background, including the spaces inside and between letters; no ground, no backdrop, no drop shadow slab, no border, no frame, no mockup, no watermark. Wide landscape canvas. The whole mark centred with generous empty margin around it. Flat-on front view, not in perspective, not on an object. It will be composited over a bright aerial photograph of grey concrete and green jungle, so it needs strong value contrast at its edges and must stay legible at a quarter of screen width.
