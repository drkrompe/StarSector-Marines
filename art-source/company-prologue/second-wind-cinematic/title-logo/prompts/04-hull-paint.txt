Use case: title logo for the end of a game cinematic. Direction: hand-painted hull lettering. Starsector Marines is a grounded game about a small mercenary marine company of refugees from a failing mining station, who arrive on a jungle world in a patched, much-repaired old freighter. The mark is the ship's own lettering: MARINES brush-painted by hand in tall, bold, slightly condensed block capitals on a section of weathered hull plating made of several overlapping patched panels with weld seams and rivet lines, in mismatched faded rust-red, grey and primer tones. The paint is cream white with a hand-painted thin black keyline, sun-faded and scuffed, with a few chips where a panel was replaced and the lettering carries on across it. STARSECTOR is smaller, painted in a simple stencil along a narrow strip above. The hull panel section is the logo shape, cut out with a slightly irregular rectangular silhouette of plate edges; everything outside the panel is transparent. Honest, worn, proud, home-made.
