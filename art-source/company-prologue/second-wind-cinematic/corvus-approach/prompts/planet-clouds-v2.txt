Asset: an equirectangular (2:1, plate carree) cloud cover map for Jangala, a warm humid jungle and ocean world, used as the density/alpha of a cloud shell on a 3D sphere in Blender. Exactly 2:1 landscape canvas, highest resolution available, full bleed, flat orthographic map projection, nothing else in the frame.
Content: greyscale clouds on PURE BLACK. Black (#000000) means clear sky and must be completely black: no land, no ocean, no colour, no texture in the gaps. Brightness encodes optical thickness: deep convective towers and thick frontal cloud near white, thin stratus and cirrus veils dark-to-mid grey. Flat and unlit: no shading direction, no shadows, no terminator.
Weather, realistic global circulation as in a real geostationary satellite composite of Earth (GOES / Himawari full-disc mosaics), but an alien world: a broken equatorial band of tropical convective clusters (anvil tops, popcorn cumulus fields, a few large mesoscale complexes) along the middle of the map; clearer subtropical belts above and below it with scattered fair-weather cumulus and some low stratocumulus decks with cellular texture; in each hemisphere's mid-latitudes two or three large comma-shaped extratropical cyclones with long trailing cold fronts that curve toward the equator, cirrus streaks along the jet; one mature tropical cyclone in the northern subtropics right of centre with tight spiral bands and a small, ragged, partly cloud-filled eye that is dark grey, never a hard black dot. Roughly 40% total coverage with large, natural clear areas. Cloud edges are soft and fibrous at the scale of a whole planet, not cotton blobs.
Projection: equirectangular, features stretch horizontally only near the top and bottom rows; no other distortion, no vignette. The left and right edges must continue seamlessly into each other (wrap-around tileable horizontally). Polar rows sparse and thin, not heavy streaked bands.
No text, grid, borders, legend or watermark.
