Asset: an equirectangular (2:1, plate carree) cloud cover map for the jungle ocean world Jangala, used as the alpha/colour of a cloud layer on a 3D sphere in Blender. Exactly 2:1 landscape canvas, highest resolution available, full bleed, flat orthographic map projection, nothing else in the frame.
Content: white and light-grey clouds on PURE BLACK. Black (#000000) means clear sky and must be completely black with no texture, no land, no ocean, no colour anywhere. Cloud brightness encodes cloud density: thick cloud near white, thin wisps grey. Greyscale only.
Weather: a stormy tropical world with severe weather. One large, well-formed cyclone with tight spiral arms and a clear eye in the northern mid-latitudes, right of centre; one smaller developing storm swirl elsewhere. Several long sweeping frontal cloud bands curving across the mid-latitudes in both hemispheres, broken cumulus fields and popcorn convection scattered over the tropics, streaky cirrus, and some large clear gaps. Roughly 40-50% coverage overall, natural and varied, like a real satellite cloud composite.
Projection: equirectangular, so features stretch horizontally only near the top and bottom rows; no globe curvature, no vignette. The left and right edges must continue seamlessly into each other (wrap-around tileable horizontally).
Flat and unlit: no shading direction, no shadows, no terminator. No text, grid, borders, legend or watermark.
