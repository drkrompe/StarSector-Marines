Asset: a high-detail regional cloud cover map for the jungle ocean world Jangala, used as the alpha and density of a cloud layer draped over the camera-facing part of a 3D planet in Blender. Exactly 2:1 landscape canvas, highest resolution available, full bleed, flat top-down view of a region about 100 degrees wide by 50 degrees tall, nothing else in the frame.
Content: white and light-grey clouds on PURE BLACK. Black (#000000) means clear sky and must be completely black: no land, no ocean, no colour, no texture in the gaps. Brightness encodes density: thick towers near white, thin wisps and veils dark grey. Greyscale only, flat and unlit: no shading direction, no shadows.
Weather on a stormy tropical world, with satellite-photo detail: one large mature cyclone with tight spiral bands and a small clear eye, its eye centred at about 62% of the width from the left and 20% of the height from the top, its arms spanning roughly a fifth of the image width. One long frontal cloud band trailing from the cyclone and sweeping in a broad curve down to the lower left, a second thinner frontal band crossing the lower right. Scattered popcorn cumulus and small convective clusters over the rest, some streaky cirrus, and generous clear black gaps so that roughly 35% of the image is cloud.
Reference 1 is cloud texture and style only (how storm bands and cumulus fields look); do not copy its layout or coverage.
No text, grid, borders, legend, vignette or watermark.
