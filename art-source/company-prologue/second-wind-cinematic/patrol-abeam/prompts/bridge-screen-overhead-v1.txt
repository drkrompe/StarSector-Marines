A flat, straight-on capture of an overhead annunciator and status display panel on the bridge of an old interplanetary freighter: monochrome amber (around #e8781e) on near-black, like a warm plasma or sodium display. A grid of small rectangular annunciator tiles, most dimly lit with short uppercase labels (HULL P, CABIN P, O2, CO2, COOLANT, RCS A, RCS B, DRIVE, GEAR, DOCK, MASTER CAUTION) and a few brightly lit, plus a strip of segmented bar readouts and a digital mission clock. Very long thin landscape canvas (about 4:1), filled edge to edge. No bezel, no panel hardware, no room, no reflections, no perspective, no watermark, no logos.
