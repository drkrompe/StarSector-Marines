## Standing decisions

- Render only what the owner has something new to judge (owner,
  2026-10-20). A ship round or pass that changes what the owner sees gets one
  small look render (one light queue job). Completeness renders do not: no
  view per hull after a shared-kit rebuild, no catalogues or turnarounds.
  Rebuilds are proven by the verify scripts, `check_detail.py`,
  `check_hardpoints.py` and IoU. Texture guide renders that feed Codex stay
  allowed, at minimum samples in as few queue jobs as possible.
- Rough shape is not a finished model (owner, 2026-10-20: "we're getting
  the rough shape right, but need to add detail"). Every visible part must
  hold up at a close view: curved forms with enough segments that no facets
  show, bevelled hard edges, recessed windows with frames and dark glass
  (never glowing rectangles), dishes, masts and lamps on believable mounts,
  and no bare primitives. Review captions name anything still at blockout
  level.
  The standard is written down in metres in `ship-detail-guide.md` (from
  the Legion and Colossus, which the owner holds up as the references),
  built by `ship-detail-kit/` and measured by `ship-detail-kit/check_detail.py`,
  which every pass-2 and pass-3 build runs before owner review (2026-10-20).
- Sizes must make logical sense relative to each other and to each hull's
  role (owner, 2026-10-20). `ship_scale.py` option C is a starting estimate,
  not a law: a reshape that changes a hull's volume does not shrink or grow
  it. A size changes only on the owner's decision, argued from role and from
  the neighbouring hulls (frigates below destroyers below cruisers, a
  tender or freighter sized for its job).
- Every hull has its own real-life scale (`metres_per_unit` in its
  `geometry.json`). Shots derive lengths from it and never borrow another
  hull's.
- Object names are identifiers: damage, surfacing, VFX and cinematics key off
  them. Engines are always `Engine {i} aft-facing bell / mouth lip / dark
  throat`.
- The Second Wind's lifecycle contract is `second-wind-pipeline.md`.
