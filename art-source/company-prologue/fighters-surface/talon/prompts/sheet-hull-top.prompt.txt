Use case: precise-object-edit / texture reskin of a technical render sheet.

bottom-right: the top of one swept wing plate, red armour, leading edge to the right, with two engraved panel lines crossing in an X.

Paint each of the four tiles in INPUT 1 as that surface of a front-line fighter in good order. This image will be cut into its four tiles and projected back onto the 3D model as a texture, so geometry must not move:
- Keep the four tiles exactly where they are, the same size, with the same plain grey gutters between them. Do not zoom, crop, shift, rotate, enlarge or shrink anything. Output: one landscape 16:9 image, same composition as INPUT 1.
