Texture reference 1, a placement guide, into one finished SEAMLESS TILEABLE texture for a top-down game: the open roof deck of a gritty industrial sky port megastructure, where infantry fight between freight lanes. Seen straight down (orthographic), soft overcast top light, no cast shadows, no lighting gradient, no vignette.

Reference 1 is the GEOMETRY AUTHORITY: keep the same square canvas and the 4x4 grid of heavy steel deck plates exactly where it is, with the dark seam lines on the same positions (half a seam on each canvas edge, so the texture repeats on a full seam), the bolts where they are, the three recessed drainage grilles and the two square hatch covers where they are. Add nothing that crosses a seam except stains.
Reference 2 is MATERIAL, PALETTE AND MOOD ONLY (the roof of a lower terrace of the same structure; never copy its layout, vents or pipes): heavy patched riveted steel, soot, rust, steel grey, faded hazard yellow.

The plates are each about 4 m square: dull grey-brown paint worn to dark steel, broad soft oil stains and soot, tyre scuffs, a few welded repair patches, rust bleeding from the grilles and bolts. Keep the surface texture broad and soft: NO fine diamond tread, no fine high-contrast speckle; detail should still read when the image is shrunk to one eighth. Even value across the whole canvas, mid-dark, so it repeats without a visible tile. Opposite edges must match exactly. No text, no numbers, no labels, no border, no lights.
