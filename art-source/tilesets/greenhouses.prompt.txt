Readable broad forms at small game scale. No chunky pixel-art outlines, no cartoon style, no text, numbers or letters anywhere.

True alpha transparency around every object and in every gap of the guide; no floor, no background, no shadow slab, no border, no labels, no watermark. Paint only these four objects and nothing outside their silhouettes.
