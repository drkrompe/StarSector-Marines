Use case: tileable liquid surface for a top-down tactical game. Image 1 is a FORMAT reference only: a shipped open-water tile showing how flat and low-contrast a liquid surface must be. Its blue colour is NOT the colour to use.
A wide square canvas filled edge to edge with one continuous pool surface, seen STRAIGHT DOWN, orthographic, with flat even overcast light: no sun glint, no reflections of sky or objects, no shore, no edge, no vignette.
The liquid: a still pool of thick acidic chemical sludge on a toxic world. Murky dark yellow-green and olive liquid with a faint oily sheen, slow swirls of paler sickly green scum and small clusters of tiny bubbles, a few drifting patches of darker brown froth. Average colour a dark murky olive-green around RGB (70, 86, 38). Murky and dull, slightly brighter and greener than the ground beside it but not neon and not glowing.
Fine detail only: nothing larger than about one twentieth of the canvas, spread evenly so any crop looks like any other. No rocks, banks, debris, plants, objects, labels, text or border.
