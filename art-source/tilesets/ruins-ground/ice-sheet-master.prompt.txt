Use case: tileable floor material for a top-down tactical game, inside an abandoned Domain-era factory hall in a spacefaring civilization. Image 1 is a FORMAT reference only: the hall's shipped concrete floor tile, showing how flat, fine and low-contrast the surface must be and the scale of its detail.
A wide square canvas filled edge to edge with one continuous floor surface, seen STRAIGHT DOWN, orthographic, with flat even light: no directional lighting, no highlights, no cast shadows, no vignette, no horizon.
The material: a thin sheet of frozen coolant over the steel and concrete deck: translucent blue-grey ice with the grey deck faintly showing through, fine internal cracks and trapped bubbles, faint white frost bloom, a few scratches. Average colour a cold pale blue-grey around RGB (150, 162, 172). Desaturated, dull, not bright white, not cyan.
Fine detail only: nothing larger than about one twentieth of the canvas, spread evenly over the whole surface so any crop of it looks like any other. No objects, machines, pipes, footprints, labels, text or border.
