Reskin the attached guide sheet into top-down fire sprites for an overhead tactical game. The camera looks STRAIGHT DOWN at the ground from high above: there are no side-view flame tongues rising up the image, no vertical flames, no horizon, no perspective. It must NOT look like an explosion, a starburst, a star, a sun, a flower or a blast: no radial spikes, no symmetric points, no bright single centre with rays.

Layout rules (strict):
- The guide is a 4x4 grid of square frames on a 1024x1024 canvas. Keep exactly that grid, each frame's fire exactly where and as big as the guide's shapes, following their outlines closely. Do not move, merge, add or remove shapes. Frames must not touch their neighbours.

- True transparent background, including all space between and around the flames. No ground, grass, soil, ash patch, shadow slab, border, grid lines, labels or text.
