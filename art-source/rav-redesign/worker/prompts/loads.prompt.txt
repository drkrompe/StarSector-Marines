Two tiny carried-load sprites for a top-down 2D game, painted side by side on one sheet: things that the SKITTERER, a small nest-worker creature of the Rav Bloom (an engineered alien swarm), carries in its mandibles. Each will be shown only about 8-12 pixels across in game.

Reference 1 is the GEOMETRY AUTHORITY: a flat colour-blocked guide. Keep both objects exactly where they are on the canvas, at the same size, with the same outer silhouette and colour layout; repaint the flat blocks as finished, volumetric painted art. Do not add a third object, do not move or merge them.

Reference 2 is the STYLE AND PALETTE AUTHORITY only (not layout): the skitterer's own body, painted pale pearly lilac chitin with dusky violet shading, deep crimson sinew and ivory bone. Match its painterly finish, its lilac, and its lighting.

LEFT object, the FEED: a glistening glob of raw biomass. A plump, rounded, slightly lumpy blob. Its core is warm glowing amber-orange, translucent and wet, with a bright specular highlight toward the upper left; deep crimson sinew strands run through it and wrap over its surface; it is held in a thin translucent pale-lilac membrane skin that forms a clear, smooth, continuous rim around the whole glob. It reads as warm, juicy organic food.

RIGHT object, the PULP: a ragged wad of chewed tree bark. Matte, dry, dead and woody: torn dark-brown bark fibres and a few short pale splintered wood shards poking out of the edge, with a few curled dead grey-brown leaves stuck in it, all bound together by a few thin pale-lilac creep veins wrapped around it like string. It reads cool, brown, dry and ragged, clearly different from the feed: no glow, no gloss, no orange. Keep it a compact wad; splinters stick out only a little.

Both: strict overhead orthographic view looking straight down, soft light from the upper left, big clear shapes and a strong dark-to-light value contrast with a clear darker rim so each silhouette survives at a few pixels; no fine noise, no texture grain, no tiny detail.

Background: keep the flat pure chroma green (#00B140) of reference 1 everywhere outside the two objects, perfectly flat and uniform. No cast shadow, no ground, no glow spill onto the background, no text, no labels, no border.
