Top-down concept for a tactical game. ONE creature, the SPITTER: the Rav Bloom swarm's fragile ranged strain (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon). It hangs back behind the melee Runners and lobs arcing globs of acid that splash infantry and can hit low drones. Original design, not StarCraft. Reference image 1 is the Runner, the same organism's melee strain: keep its tissue, palette and finish exactly (pale lilac and mauve glossy chitin plates worn as armour over muscled flesh, deep purple-magenta sinew in the gaps, ivory claws and spurs, a pale spine ridge of plates, small amber eyes). Beast first, chitin as armour, never insect-dominant; no wings, no antennae. This strain stands on TWO or THREE legs, not four, and is bilaterally SYMMETRIC (no lopsided growths). Its acid is a DEEP, DARK VENOM GREEN, dark bottle-green that glows from inside (not lime, not neon): the acid organ is translucent and shows that dark green glow through it. No forward arm blades.

This concept: SIPHON-ARMED BIPED. A tall, upright biped whose two forelimbs, where the Runner carries blades, have become a pair of long hollow ivory bone SIPHON TUBES pointing forward and up, fed from a translucent acid sac in the chest. It FIRES FROM THE ARMS: it raises both siphons together and the glob is squirted out of their tips.

Strict overhead orthographic view, camera looking straight down at its back, standing at rest, facing UP (head toward the top), centred, filling about two thirds of the frame, light from the upper left. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no shadow, no ground, no projectile, no text, no border.
