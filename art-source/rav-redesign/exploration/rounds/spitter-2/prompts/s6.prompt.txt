Concept art for a tactical game. ONE creature, the SPITTER: the Rav Bloom swarm's fragile ranged strain (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon). It hangs back behind the melee Runners and lobs arcing globs of acid that splash infantry and can hit low drones. Original design, not StarCraft. Reference image 1 is the Runner, the same organism's melee strain: keep its tissue, palette and finish exactly (pale lilac and mauve glossy chitin plates worn as armour over muscled flesh, deep purple-magenta sinew in the gaps, ivory claws and spurs, a pale spine ridge of plates, small amber eyes). Beast first, chitin as armour, never insect-dominant; no wings, no antennae. This strain stands on TWO or THREE legs, not four, and is bilaterally SYMMETRIC (no lopsided growths). Its acid is a DEEP, DARK VENOM GREEN, dark bottle-green that glows from inside (not lime, not neon): the acid organ is translucent and shows that dark green glow through it. No forward arm blades.

This concept: VULTURE SPITTER. A hunched biped like a vulture or a heron crossed with a hound: strong legs, a long muscular S-curved neck, and a long translucent gular pouch hanging under the neck full of acid. The head is narrow with a tube-like snout. It FIRES FROM THE SNOUT: it snaps its neck forward and up like a whip and flings the glob from the snout.

SIDE VIEW concept of the same creature in reference image 2 (keep its design exactly), seen in profile facing RIGHT, caught at the instant it FIRES: braced, the acid organ visibly squeezing, a single glob of dark glowing green acid just leaving the firing opening on a high upward arc toward the upper right. The firing mechanism must be clear. Full body visible, centred, wide landscape canvas. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no ground line, no shadow, no text, no border.
