Top-down concept for a tactical game (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon, the Rav Bloom). ONE creature, the SPITTER: the swarm's fragile ranged strain, which hangs back behind the Runners and lobs arcing globs of acid that splash infantry and can hit low drones. Original design, not StarCraft. Reference image 1 is the Runner, the same organism's fast melee strain: keep its identity, palette and finish exactly (pale lilac and mauve glossy chitin plates worn as armour over a muscled predator beast body, deep purple-magenta sinew in the gaps, ivory tips and spurs, a pale spine ridge, small amber eyes). Beast first, chitin as armour, never insect-dominant; no bug wings, no zooid colony bodies. It must read at a glance as "the ranged one" and NOT as the Runner: no forward-pointing arm blades, a different silhouette built around its ACID ORGAN, which is translucent and glows from inside so it is the brightest thing on the body. Asymmetry and depth over a balanced, mirrored design. Strict overhead orthographic view, camera looking straight down at its back, facing UP (head toward the top), centred, filling about two thirds of the frame, light from the upper left. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no shadow, no ground, no acid puddle, no projectile, no text, no border.

This concept: TAIL LAUNCHER. A sleek beast whose long, segmented, armoured tail is carried curled up and forward over its back, scorpion-like but on a mammal-like predator body, its end a heavy translucent bulb glowing toxic acid lime-green, veined magenta. The curl sits off to the right of the spine. Low wide hind legs braced, slim forequarters, small head.
