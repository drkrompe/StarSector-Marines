Concept art for a tactical game. ONE creature, the SPITTER: the Rav Bloom swarm's fragile ranged strain (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon). It hangs back behind the melee Runners and lobs arcing globs of acid that splash infantry and can hit low drones. Original design, not StarCraft. It must look clearly DIFFERENT from the Runner (reference image 1, a pale lilac blade-armed hound): a different colour scheme and a different, heavier, rounder body mass, so a player tells them apart at a glance from far above. It is still the same engineered organism, so keep only the tissue STRUCTURE of reference 1 (armour plates over muscled flesh, sinew in the gaps, bone claws), NOT its colours. Beast first, never insect-dominant; no wings, no antennae, no forward arm blades. It stands on TWO or THREE legs and is bilaterally SYMMETRIC. Its acid is a DEEP, DARK VENOM GREEN that glows from inside a translucent acid organ.

COLOURS: BANDED: dark plum carapace with bold ivory bone bands across the back plates, a strong striped pattern from above; magenta sinew; dark green glowing dome.

BODY AND FIRING: A radially braced tripod: one sturdy leg forward under the head and two legs splayed back at the hips. A big round domed acid bladder sits on top of the body, capped by a ring of plates around an upward-facing vent; it clenches the dome and fires the glob straight up from the vent like a living mortar.

SIDE VIEW concept of the same creature in reference image 2 (keep its design and colours exactly), seen in profile facing RIGHT, caught at the instant it FIRES: braced, the acid organ visibly squeezing, a single glob of dark glowing green acid just leaving the firing opening on a high upward arc toward the upper right. The firing mechanism must be clear. Full body visible, centred, wide landscape canvas. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no ground line, no shadow, no text, no border.
