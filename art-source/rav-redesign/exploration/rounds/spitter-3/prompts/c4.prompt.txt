Top-down concept for a tactical game. ONE creature, the SPITTER: the Rav Bloom swarm's fragile ranged strain (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon). It hangs back behind the melee Runners and lobs arcing globs of acid that splash infantry and can hit low drones. Original design, not StarCraft. It must look clearly DIFFERENT from the Runner (reference image 1, a pale lilac blade-armed hound): a different colour scheme and a different, heavier, rounder body mass, so a player tells them apart at a glance from far above. It is still the same engineered organism, so keep only the tissue STRUCTURE of reference 1 (armour plates over muscled flesh, sinew in the gaps, bone claws), NOT its colours. Beast first, never insect-dominant; no wings, no antennae, no forward arm blades. It stands on TWO or THREE legs and is bilaterally SYMMETRIC. Its acid is a DEEP, DARK VENOM GREEN that glows from inside a translucent acid organ.

COLOURS: deep INDIGO and blue-violet carapace (cool, dark, glossy), teal-tinged sinew, dark green glowing reservoir. Ivory claws.

BODY AND FIRING: A squat, very wide toad-like biped, legs splayed out to the sides as braces, a broad flat head. On its back a round reservoir with twin short bone mortar tubes side by side. It squats and pumps two globs at once out of the twin tubes.

Strict overhead orthographic view, camera looking straight down at its back, standing at rest, facing UP (head toward the top), centred, filling about two thirds of the frame, light from the upper left. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no shadow, no ground, no projectile, no text, no border.
