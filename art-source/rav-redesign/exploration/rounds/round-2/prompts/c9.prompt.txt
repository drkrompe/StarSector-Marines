Top-down concept for a tactical game (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon). ONE creature, the Runner: the swarm's fast hunter. A BEAST first and an insect second: a four-legged predator with a dog or big-cat frame, a thick muscular neck and shoulders, a deep chest, NO thin waist, strong haunches, legs that read as muscled limbs (not thin jointed sticks), held out in a wide mid-stride lope. Chitin is ARMOUR laid over that body, not the whole anatomy: a shoulder shield, a line of spine plates, a banded armoured tail, with dark leathery hide or membrane showing between the plates. A monster's head: a fanged maw or heavy jaws (small mandibles at most), small amber eye clusters, no antennae. On its back, jointed striking spikes that arch forward over the head, cocked to stab down past it. Strict overhead orthographic view, camera looking straight down at its back, facing UP (head toward the top), centred, filling about two thirds of the frame. Near-black walnut and dark brown chitin plates with pale cream edges, a crisp lacquer sheen, light from the upper left; dark hide between plates. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no shadow, no ground, no text, no border.
