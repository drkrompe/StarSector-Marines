DESIGN B, "PLUM MANTA": a broad, flat ray-like flier whose wings are part of the body. Darker plum-violet carapace with pale lilac on plate edges and the spine ridge (like an older Rav), crimson sinew in the gaps. The body widens straight into two broad fleshy wing lobes armoured along their leading edge with overlapping plates and bone hooks on the wing tips; the trailing edge is crimson membrane. A short blunt armoured head with the Runner's fanged jaw, two short clawed forelimbs tucked under the chin, and a long whip tail of plates ending in a barbed bone stinger. Wingspan about twice the body length; the silhouette is a wide diamond, very different from the four-legged ground strains.
