DESIGN C, "BLOOD SCYTHE": the Matriarch's brood, in her palette. Charcoal-black carapace plates with dried-blood red highlights on edges and ridges (reference image 2 shows the palette; take only its colours and finish), crimson sinew between plates. Each wing is a rigid fan of four long overlapping bone-and-carapace blades like scythes, splayed with dark crimson membrane slits between them, the outermost blade tip a hooked ivory point. A compact muscular torso, a narrow plated head with the Runner's fanged jaw, two forward-reaching clawed forelimbs, and a short forked tail of two bone spikes. Wingspan about twice the body length; the silhouette is sharp and jagged, like a thrown blade.
