Top-down concept for a tactical game (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon, the Rav Bloom). ONE creature, the BRUTE: the swarm's slow, heavily armoured melee strain that smashes through doors, windows and light walls and soaks gunfire. It is the SAME ORGANISM as the Runner in reference image 1, at a heavier, older growth stage: keep the Runner's identity, palette and finish exactly (pale lilac and mauve glossy carapace plates, deep purple-magenta sinew and muscle showing in every gap, ivory bone blades and claw tips, small hot amber eyes), but the body is a massive, low, heavy beast roughly twice the Runner's bulk: beast first with chitin as armour over a muscled body, NOT an insect, no antennae, no extra spindly legs. Its armour is THICK overlapping plates, calcified and paler at the edges, some edges chipped and cracked (the plate is what gunfire wears away). It must read at a glance as something that goes THROUGH walls. Avoid a balanced symmetrical design where the brief allows: asymmetry and depth. Strict overhead orthographic view, camera looking straight down at its back, facing UP (head toward the top), centred, filling about two thirds of the frame, light from the upper left. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no shadow, no ground, no text, no border.

This concept: RHINO RAM. A rhino-like quadruped, low and wide, all mass forward. The Runner's two back blades have fused into one massive forward-swept battering horn that runs from the shoulders over the skull and juts past the snout, a calcified ivory ram-cap on its front. Short thick legs, broad flat back plates like stacked shingles. Slightly asymmetric: the horn curves a little to its left and the right shoulder plate is heavier.
