Top-down concept for a tactical game (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon, the Rav Bloom). ONE creature, the BRUTE: the swarm's slow, heavily armoured ram that smashes through doors, windows and light walls. Reference image 1 is the chosen Brute design: keep its identity (a rhino-like heavy quadruped whose two back blades have fused into one massive forward-swept battering horn running from the shoulders over the skull, with a calcified ivory ram-cap jutting past the snout; thick overlapping plates, chipped at the edges; slight asymmetry, the horn curving a little to its left). Reference image 2 is the Runner, the same organism at a young stage: palette family only. This pass: (a) the Brute is OLDER than the Runner, so its carapace has DARKENED WITH AGE, as the concept states below, while staying in the same purple family with magenta-crimson sinew in the gaps, ivory bone horn and claws, small amber eyes; no outline rim around the body; (b) its legs and body are a cross between a BOAR and a BULL: four thick, heavily muscled legs with hooves-like armoured feet ending in short ivory claws, deep chest, massive humped shoulders and neck, short powerful hindquarters, NOT insect legs, NOT a reptile sprawl, legs set under the body like a charging bull. Beast first, chitin as armour over muscle. Strict overhead orthographic view, camera looking straight down at its back, facing UP (head toward the top), centred, filling about two thirds of the frame, light from the upper left. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no shadow, no ground, no text, no border.
