Top-down concept for a tactical game (Starsector mod: Marines against an engineered biological weapon, the Rav Bloom). ONE creature, the BRUTE: the swarm's slow, heavily armoured ram that smashes through doors, windows and light walls. Reference image 1 is the chosen Brute: keep its identity, palette and finish exactly (an old, heavy quadruped; deep dark plum-violet carapace plates with lilac only on worn, chipped edges and ridges; magenta-crimson sinew in the gaps; ivory bone; small amber eyes; a pale ridge of spine plates; no outline rim). Reference image 2 is the young Runner: palette family only. LEGS, important: this is a hoofed, cow- or pig-like walker, NOT a lizard or alligator. Its four legs stand straight DOWN UNDER the body, so seen from directly above the wide armoured body hides almost all of them: only the front of the knees and the armoured hooves with short ivory claws peek out just past the body's outline, close in to the flanks, never splayed sideways. The body is a deep, wide barrel, broadest at the shoulders. THE HEAD is the subject of this pass: the Brute rams through walls head first, and its head is its weapon, as the concept describes below; make the head big, pale ivory bone against the dark body, and readable as a shape. Strict overhead orthographic view, camera looking straight down at its back, facing UP (head toward the top), centred, filling about two thirds of the frame, light from the upper left. Plain flat light grey background (#b4b4b4), no shadow, no ground, no text, no border.

This concept: CHISEL HORN: the round-2 forward horn kept, but flattened into a broad chisel with serrated blade edges along both sides, and a squared cutting tip.
