# Bombard art (Rav artillery strain)

The Bombard (`bombard.md`, the Rav long arm). The picked look is
`raw/bombard5-withered.png`, two frames side by side: the wind-up (tail cocked
over the back with the loaded sling) and the release (tail flung out behind,
glob in flight). Lobster build, heavy charcoal carapace edged in dried-blood
red, the back shell split over a glowing amber bile sac, thick walking legs,
withered claws, short antennae.

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `raw/bombard5-withered.png` | The pick (G1), generated as a revision of its parent |
| `raw/bombard4-armoured.png` | Its parent (G): the same body with full claws and long antennae |
| `prompts/*.prompt.txt` | The briefs for both, as sent |

Each master has its `.provenance.json`. The other rounds (gland launchers, soft
bodies, moult) were rejected and are kept on Drive under
`review/rav-artillery/`, not here.
