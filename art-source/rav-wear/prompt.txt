A sheet of twelve battle-damage decals for an overhead (straight top-down) alien creature sprite: organic wear marks
laid over the creature's armoured chitin as it is shot. The creature is a huge armoured beast of matte charcoal chitin
plates edged in dried-blood red over wine-red sinew. Painted, slightly gritty realistic game-art style, matching the
creature reference. This is flesh and shell, never metal: no bare steel, no rivets, no scorch, no sparks.

Layout: reference 1 is the exact layout. It is a 4 x 3 grid of equal square cells on a transparent canvas, and each
flat grey shape is the footprint of one decal. Fill every grey footprint with its decal, keeping its position, size,
outline and orientation. Do not move, merge, add or remove marks. Keep every cell's empty space fully transparent.

Rows, top to bottom (each row is one stage of wear; the four columns are variants of it):
1. Scored and chipped chitin. Scores are gouged lines where rounds skidded across the shell, cutting through the dark
   outer layer to a paler bone-grey and dull ivory under-layer, with a thin dark edge and flaked shell at the ends.
   Chips are small round strikes: a shallow starred crater of pale cracked shell with flakes broken out round it.
2. Bruising and cracked plate. Soft, irregular dark bruises in the shell: a deep purple-black and dark plum centre
   fading out through dull maroon, with a fine web of hairline cracks across it and a little dark ichor seeping from
   the cracks. Soft feathered alpha at the rim.
3. Weeping wounds. The shell torn through: broken, lifted chitin plate edges with pale fractured rims around a wet
   opening of raw crimson sinew and dark glistening ichor. Where a thin tail leaves a wound, it is a glossy run of
   dark red ichor ending in a bead. Wet highlights from the upper left.

Lighting from the upper left, matching the creature reference.

Readability matters most: each mark is shrunk to about 40 pixels across and laid over a body that is already charcoal
and wine-red, so a mark in those same colours disappears. Paint every mark with strong value contrast against charcoal
and dark red, and keep its shapes bold and few rather than fine:
- scores and chips are bright bone-white and pale ivory where the shell is cut, with a crisp black edge;
- bruises are a sickly discolouration that no healthy part of the body has: jaundiced yellow-olive at the rim
  fading to a mottled violet centre, crossed by bold dark cracks with bright ivory edges;
- wounds are glossy and wet: a black cavity, bright scarlet and crimson flesh with white specular highlights, and
  pale bone-ivory broken plate lifting at the lips; the ichor run is a bright glossy scarlet.
Never use the body's own charcoal or wine-red as a mark's main colour.

Output: a true transparent background, including every gap between and inside marks; no creature, no shell plate
backing, no ground, no cast shadow slab, no grid lines, no border, no text or labels. Each decal must stay inside its
own cell.

Reference 2 is material and style only: the creature's own body sprite from the same game, for the look of its
chitin, plate edges and sinew. Do not draw the creature or copy its layout.
