Aircraft ImageGen up-res masters and runtime export

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool from the six existing FighterProfile
sprites in the installed Starsector core. Native dimensions, authored pivots,
source paths, selected masters and exact prompt files are in manifest.json.

The approved masters feed the six FighterProfile battle textures under
mod/graphics/battle/aircraft. The generated masters remain unchanged here.
pack.ps1 exports lossless RGBA PNGs at eight times the native frame dimensions
(144-344 pixels per side, about 482 KiB combined; budget 1 MiB). The large masters,
previous iterations, prompts and review sheets do not ship.

The exporter registers the meaningful painted hull bounds to the original
painted hull bounds, using the retained alpha >= 32 measurements in manifest.json.
The bounds are registration landmarks, not a transparency threshold: the full
RGBA image is resampled through that two-axis transform into the original frame.
Faint stray pixels do not determine the transform. Talon and Longbow require
aspect correction; Dagger also needs its painted width restored to the native
frame. Output aspect always matches the original hull, including its margins.
Native hull identity still determines physical size, pivot, weapons and handling.

Talon v3 restores its offset gun and cockpit; Longbow v2 restores the unequal
forward launchers; Dagger v2 restores the forked nose and unequal ordnance.
Wasp v2 reduces the rear perspective of its engine openings. Earlier versions
are retained with their prompts for comparison. The exporter corrects master canvas padding and
proportions before runtime use; ImageGen detail
reconstruction is not an exact geometric upscale of the native pixel mask.
After frame registration, Wasp and Longbow's painted nozzles differ from the
original engine locations. FighterProfile supplies render-only nozzle offsets
for these two appearances, in native pixels (+X right, +Y nose), in original
engine order: Wasp [(2,-1.25),(-2,-1.25)], Longbow [(-2.5,0.5),(3,0.5)].
The engine physics, demand ordering, plume direction, size and style remain native.

render-review.ps1 lays out original and selected draft at the same display size
per pair. All source pixels and alpha are unchanged; the sheet merely draws
whole images and labels. Panel display sizes are for art review, not relative
gameplay scale. Re-run it with PowerShell; StarsectorCore can select the source
installation. review.png is the resulting 1920x1080 comparison.

runtime-review.png is the production renderer's common-scale six-aircraft and
three-turret lineup at 60 px/cell, from createSnapshots -Psnapshot=missile-aa-scale.
It uses the compact shipped textures, native hull geometry and engine slots.
