Style: detailed late-1990s/early-2000s science-fiction strategy-game illustration, matte-painted cinematic industrial realism, visible restrained brush texture, the same painted family as the game's character portraits. Tactile worn metal, scuffed deck plate, cable runs, patched paint. Restrained olive, charcoal, umber and cream palette. Lighting, which the figure layers will match: warm amber key light from below and in front, coming from console screens and the chart table; soft cool blue-grey fill from a long viewport on the back wall; dim ceiling.
