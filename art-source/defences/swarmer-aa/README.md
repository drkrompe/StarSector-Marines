# Swarmer ground AA head

First direct built-in ImageGen up-res of the vanilla Swarmer SRM Launcher:
four forward tubes, red housing, pale painted marking and asymmetric side details.

- [Generated master](mount.master.png): unmodified 1254-square transparent output.
- [Exact prompt](mount.prompt.txt).
- [512-square RGBA head](layers/mount.png) and [tower composite](composite.png).
- `sources/swarmer_srm_launcher_turret_base.png`: unmodified 24-square vanilla
  turret sprite; `swarmer.wpn` records weapon id `swarmer` and its original mounts.

See the [family documentation](../missile-aa/README.md) for references, preview
registration and export instructions. The center pivot is provisional art
placement; gameplay and firing origins are not authored by this pass.
