# Proximity Charge ground AA head

First direct built-in ImageGen up-res of the vanilla Proximity Charge Launcher:
split olive housings, purple center and side panels, dark channels and open frame.

- [Generated master](mount.master.png): unmodified 1254-square transparent output.
- [Exact prompt](mount.prompt.txt).
- [512-square RGBA head](layers/mount.png) and [tower composite](composite.png).
- `sources/phase_charge_launcher_turret_base.png`: unmodified 40-square vanilla
  turret sprite; `phasecl.wpn` records weapon id `phasecl` and its original mounts.

See the [family documentation](../missile-aa/README.md) for references, preview
registration and export instructions. Internal channel details are generated
interpretations. The center pivot is provisional art placement; gameplay and
firing origins are not authored by this pass.
