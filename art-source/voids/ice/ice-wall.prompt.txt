A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective, no sky, no horizon) of the sheer wall of a
glacial crevasse, as it would be mapped onto a vertical cliff of ice.
Old glacier ice: horizontal layered strata of blue-white firn and clear
blue ice running across the tile in bands of uneven thickness, some bands
milky white with trapped air, some glassy turquoise-blue, a few thin dark
grey bands of wind-blown grit and rock dust. Vertical meltwater streaks and
runnels of smoother glazed ice running down across the strata, small icicle
fringes under the overhanging bands, fine fracture lines and a few
conchoidal chips. Lit evenly by soft neutral light from straight in front,
no cast shadows from outside the tile, no bright sky glare. Even density
across the whole tile with no focal point, so it repeats horizontally and
vertically. Palette: blue-white, pale cyan, glacier turquoise, slate blue,
a little grey grit. No snow surface, no sky, no people, no text, no
border, no vignette. Square canvas.
