A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective, no sky, no horizon) of a sheer gorge wall
cut by a river, as it would be mapped onto a vertical cliff. Weathered
sedimentary rock in horizontal strata: thick grey-brown sandstone beds
and thinner dark shale bands, each course roughly 1 to 3 m tall, with
crisp bedding lines, vertical joints and fractures, small overhanging
ledges with tufts of moss and fern, hanging roots, dark seep stains and
mineral streaks running down, lichen patches. Lit evenly by soft daylight
from straight in front, no cast shadows from outside the tile. Even
density across the whole tile with no focal point, strata running
straight across so it repeats both horizontally and vertically. Palette:
warm grey-brown, ochre, slate, dark olive moss. No water, no sky, no
people, no text, no border, no vignette. Square canvas.
