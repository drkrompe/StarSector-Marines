A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective, no sky, no horizon) of the sheer wall of a
gorge on a toxic world, as it would be mapped onto a vertical cliff.
Corroded, chemically stained bedrock: horizontal strata running across the
tile in bands of uneven thickness, dark olive-grey and khaki rock eaten
into pitted, etched ledges by acid; bright sulphur-yellow mineral crusts
caked along the ledges and seams; sickly yellow-green and chartreuse stains
weeping down across the strata from seeps, with rust-orange and ochre
leaching streaks beside them; patches of white and pale green crystalline
efflorescence; a few dark wet seeps where sludge has run down. Fine
fractures, small blistered pits. Lit evenly by soft neutral light from
straight in front, no cast shadows from outside the tile, no glow, no
bright glare. Even density across the whole tile with no focal point, so
it repeats horizontally and vertically. Palette: olive grey, khaki,
sulphur yellow, chartreuse, rust orange, dirty white. No sky, no plants, no
people, no text, no border, no vignette. Square canvas.
