A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective, no sky, no horizon) of the sheer wall of a
desert canyon, as it would be mapped onto a vertical cliff of sandstone.
Layered sedimentary sandstone: horizontal strata running across the tile in
bands of uneven thickness, alternating rust red, burnt orange, ochre, pale
cream and a few thin dark maroon and chocolate-brown shale bands; some bands
cross-bedded with fine slanted laminations, some harder bands standing out
as small overhanging ledges with a crumbling lip, softer bands weathered
back. Dark desert-varnish streaks staining down across the strata in
places, fine vertical joints and cracks, a few honeycomb weathering pits,
a little loose sand caught on the ledges. Lit evenly by soft neutral light
from straight in front, no cast shadows from outside the tile, no bright
glare. Even density across the whole tile with no focal point, so it
repeats horizontally and vertically. Palette: rust red, terracotta, ochre,
sand cream, maroon, dusty brown. No sky, no plants, no people, no text, no
border, no vignette. Square canvas.
