A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective, no sky, no horizon) of a blasted bench face
in an open-cut ore mine, as it would be mapped onto a vertical quarry wall.
Hard grey and rust-brown ore-bearing rock broken by blasting: angular
fracture planes, blocky slabs, half-barrel drill traces (vertical parallel
grooves of presplit drill holes about every metre), iron-oxide staining in
ochre and rust, pale rock-dust coating, small loose scree lodged in cracks,
faint horizontal banding of the ore body. No vegetation. Lit evenly by soft
daylight from straight in front, no cast shadows from outside the tile.
Even density across the whole tile with no focal point, so it repeats both
horizontally and vertically. Palette: dusty grey, slate, rust, ochre, a
little dark iron. No machines, no water, no sky, no people, no text, no
border, no vignette. Square canvas.
