A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective, no sky, no horizon) of the sheer wall of a
dry rock gorge on a barren airless world, as it would be mapped onto a
vertical cliff. Grey regolith and bedrock: horizontal strata running across
the tile in bands of uneven thickness, compacted grey dust and gravel
layers, darker basalt-grey lava-flow bands, pale ash-grey tuff, a few thin
rust-brown iron-stained seams; harder bands jutting as blocky fractured
ledges, softer bands crumbling back into loose scree. Angular blocky
fracturing, impact-shattered rock with sharp edges, fine vertical cracks,
a little loose grit caught on the ledges. Bone dry: no water stains, no
moss, no plants, no ice. Lit evenly by soft neutral light from straight in
front, no cast shadows from outside the tile, no bright glare. Even density
across the whole tile with no focal point, so it repeats horizontally and
vertically. Palette: neutral grey, warm grey, charcoal, ash, a little rust
brown. No sky, no people, no text, no border, no vignette. Square canvas.
