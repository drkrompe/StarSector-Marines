A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective, no sky, no horizon) of a sheer volcanic
rift wall above a lava river, as it would be mapped onto a vertical cliff.
Dark basalt: tall columnar joints, hexagonal columns roughly 1 to 2 m
wide standing vertically and broken into stacked blocks, between bands of
rough vesicular lava rock and thin layers of reddish-brown scoria and ash.
Glassy black obsidian veins, sulphur-yellow and rust mineral crusts in
the cracks, pale grey ash dusting the ledges. The rock itself is cold and
unlit: no glowing lava, no orange light, no fire in the texture (the game
adds that light). Lit evenly by soft neutral light from straight in
front, no cast shadows from outside the tile. Even density across the
whole tile with no focal point, so it repeats horizontally and
vertically. Palette: charcoal, blue-black, dark umber, rust, a little
sulphur yellow. No sky, no people, no text, no border, no vignette.
Square canvas.
