A seamless, tileable texture for a game, seen straight down (top-down,
orthographic, no perspective, no horizon): the surface of a slow river of
molten lava crusting over as it cools. Irregular plates of black and
dark charcoal cooled crust, wrinkled ropy pahoehoe folds and rough
broken slabs, from fist-sized fragments to plates several metres across,
separated by a branching network of narrow-to-wide cracks and seams of
incandescent molten rock glowing bright yellow-white at their centres,
through orange to deep red at their edges. Some plates are thin and
show a dull red glow through them. Strong contrast: the crust is near
black, the cracks are brilliant. Even density across the whole tile with
no focal point and no flow direction emphasised, so it repeats both
horizontally and vertically. No smoke, no flames above the surface, no
rocks or banks, no sky, no people, no text, no border, no vignette.
Square canvas.
