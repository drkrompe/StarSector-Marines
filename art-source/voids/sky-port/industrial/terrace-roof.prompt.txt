A seamless, tileable texture for a top-down game: the flat roof of a
terrace on a gritty industrial megastructure, seen straight down (true
overhead, no perspective, no horizon). Patched and riveted steel deck
plates, stained concrete, grilles, square vent housings with fans, pipe
runs and conduit trays, small hatch covers, faded hazard-yellow edge
lines and grime. Everything at the scale of a 40 m square of roof: a vent
housing is about 3 m across. Even density across the whole tile with no
focal point and no large single object, so it can repeat. Overcast,
diffuse light, soft contact shadows only. Palette: soot, rust, steel
grey, hazard yellow, a few small warm lights. No text, no logos, no
people, no vehicles, no border, no vignette. Square canvas.
