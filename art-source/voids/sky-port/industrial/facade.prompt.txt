A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation
(orthographic, no perspective) of the outer wall of a gritty industrial
megastructure, as it would be mapped onto a vertical wall. Heavy riveted
armour plates and girders in horizontal courses about one storey (4 m)
tall, vertical pipe runs and cable trunking, narrow lit slit windows in
warm amber, vents, maintenance ladders and small catwalk ledges, soot
streaks running down, rust and patched panels, a few red warning lights.
Even density across the whole tile with no focal point, so it repeats
both horizontally and vertically. Palette: soot, rust, steel grey,
hazard yellow, amber light. No text, no logos, no people, no border, no
vignette. Square canvas.
