A seamless, tileable background plate for a top-down game: an endless
gritty industrial hive city seen from directly overhead (true nadir, no
horizon, no tilt) from high above. Dense packed hive stacks, factory
roofs with big fan housings, tanks, pipe galleries, narrow canyon
streets glowing with warm amber light, faint red beacons, steam plumes.
Thick grey industrial haze lies over all of it, so it is low contrast and
reads as far below. A whole city block is about 60 pixels across. Even
density with no focal point, so it can repeat. Overcast daylight. No
text, no logos, no border, no vignette. Square canvas.
