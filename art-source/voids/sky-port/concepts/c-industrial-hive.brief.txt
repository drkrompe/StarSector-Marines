Direction C, industrial hive spire (gritty frontier sector): a brutal,
patched megastructure of grimy plate and girders. Massive buttresses and
docking arms with cranes and gantries bristle off its flanks below the
deck; cargo lifts, pipe runs and radiator fins cling to the walls; warning
beacons blink. Neighbouring hive stacks crowd close, connected by cable
bridges and conduits. Overcast daylight, rust, soot and steel grey with
hazard yellow; thick industrial haze over a dense city far below.
