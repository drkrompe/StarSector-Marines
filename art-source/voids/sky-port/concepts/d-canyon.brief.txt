Direction D, megacity canyon: the sky port deck stands among a cluster of
megastructures of different heights packed close around it, so the drop is
a deep urban canyon. Their roofs, terraces and edges are visible at many
depths below the deck, stacked layer on layer, each layer hazier than the
last, with sky-bridges, hanging gardens and lit windows. Deep perspective
canyon walls fall toward a glowing street level far below. Daylight
filtering down through layers of haze, cool blues with warm window light.
