Direction B, spire sky port (Star Wars city-planet): the deck is the crown
of a tall, elegant spire. Round landing platforms on slender arms jut out
from the structure at several levels below the deck, each with its own
markings and parked craft. Ribbed buttresses run down the faces. Streams of
air traffic flow in lanes between neighbouring towers at different depths.
Bright, pale stone and steel, warm afternoon haze, the city a sunlit
blue-grey sea of towers far below.
