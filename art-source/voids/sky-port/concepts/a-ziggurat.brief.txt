Direction A, arcology ziggurat (Blade Runner): the sky port is the summit of
a vast stepped pyramid. Below the deck the walls step outward in broad
terraces, each terrace its own lit roof with machinery, flare stacks
burning, vents and running lights, receding in tiers toward the city. Dark,
smoggy amber-and-teal palette; a sodium-lit city choked with haze; spotlight
beams and the glow of advertising far below. Neighbouring arcology
pyramids rise to either side at lower heights.
