A seamless, tileable texture for a game: a flat, straight-on elevation (orthographic, no perspective, no horizon) of the refractory wall of a channel in an abandoned Domain-era industrial foundry, as it would be mapped onto a vertical wall dropping into molten metal.
Geometry: reference 1 is the brick layout authority: keep every course and every brick exactly where it is, at its size, running bond, sixteen courses of eight bricks, so the pattern repeats exactly across every edge.
Material: worn yellow-buff and pale rust firebrick, heat-cracked and glazed dark brown and black in places, soot-streaked, spalled corners, dark grey mortar joints, a few rust stains running down. Old, long cold.
Light: unlit, flat and even, as if lit softly from straight in front; no glow, no orange light, no fire, no gradient or vignette (the game adds the melt's light). No cast shadows from outside the tile.
Palette: buff, ochre, rust, soot black, dark grey. Square canvas. No text, no border, no frame.
