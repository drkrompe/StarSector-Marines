DAMAGE STATE: WOUNDED. This is the same structure as the undamaged painting in the identity reference, after it has taken heavy fire and lost about half its strength. Keep its painting identity exactly: the same plates, tusks, sacs, slabs and roots in the same places, the same colours, the same glossy hand-painted rendering, the same sun from the upper left. Show the damage the guide adds, and only that: where the guide shows a plate replaced by a low dusky-mauve patch with a jagged stump at its root, the plate has been torn out of its socket, leaving raw wet sinew and torn membrane, the broken stump's edge splintered and pale; where the guide cuts a dark groove across carapace or a sac, it is a fresh gash through the shell showing wet flesh beneath, edges cracked and chipped; a sac shown deflated with a raw crater at its crown has burst, its skin sagging and torn open, a little dark ichor around the wound; a short tusk or spine is snapped off, its broken end raw bone. The amber glow is weaker and fewer: the creature is hurt, not dead. It must still read at a glance as the same living structure, now visibly wounded.
