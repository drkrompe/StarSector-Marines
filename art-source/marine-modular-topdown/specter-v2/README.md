# Specter shoulder separation

The accepted v1 raw source remains the authority for both original steel
shoulder caps, central torso, helmet and boot. `geometry.json` defines the
authored cap boundaries and mechanical inner hinge pivots in that source.
`make_guide.py` produces the measured cut guide, full-source grayscale cutting
masks, and exact body reference crop. These are geometry references rather
than repainted finished art.

The body source crop remains `[100,269,737,672]`: 637x403 pixels normalized
once to the existing 150x95 canvas. Each shoulder retains that same complete
canvas. Independent trimming would require another registration mapping and
would make the accepted idle silhouette harder to compare. The torso retains
its 1.12 master scale and Y=-0.06sw; the helmet and feet retain v1 mastering.

Left and right hinge coordinates are `[269,410]` and `[568,410]`. Their
normalized pivots are respectively `[0.2653061224,0.3498759305]` and
`[0.7346938776,0.3498759305]`. The geometric offset relative to the torso is
`[-0.2346938776,0.0950785773]` or `[0.2346938776,0.0950785773]`.
Those offsets undo the changed pivot at idle exactly; the forward-Y term
uses the final 95/150 canvas aspect. Authored layers use absolute actor-space
transforms, with scale 1.12 and mastered hinge offsets
`[-0.2628571429,0.0464880066]` and `[0.2628571429,0.0464880066]`.
Pose tracks carry the torso displacement explicitly, keeping the shoulders
registered to its motion. Paint order is torso, shoulders, helmet.

The original lower ribbed bellows remain torso pixels. The hidden dark
undersuit under each movable cap is new artwork requested with
`backing.prompt.txt`; retain the accepted raw ImageGen result and record its
measured registration before exporting. Only the backing under the cap cuts
may contribute generated pixels. Central torso, socket, outer silhouette,
original steel caps, head and foot cannot be replaced by the backing pass.
The geometric overlap target is 40 source pixels around the cuts, enough for
limited shoulder travel of +/-0.04sw and rotation of +/-8 degrees around
their inner hinges. Actual production composition must inspect both motion
extremes and braced weapon occlusion before accepting that envelope.

No shipped output is generated by the guide. Run
`python art-source/marine-modular-topdown/specter-v2/export_assets.py` to
export the retained default and three paint palettes. `build_variants.py`
delegates Specter to this exporter. `backing-source.png` is the unchanged raw
ImageGen output; its 1578x997 canvas and `[1,0,1578,989]` substantive-alpha
crop are recorded in `backing-regions.json`.

The exporter reproduces whole-part v1 palette ramps before separating caps,
so splitting cannot change casing ramp percentiles. It reuses the accepted
v1 source, cuts and casing masks, validates source fingerprints, and prepares
all palette outputs before writing. The original helmet and boot exports
remain byte-identical. Cap RGB and alpha are unchanged inside their cuts;
central torso pixels remain unchanged outside the cuts.

Backing contributes only under original cap pixels with alpha at least 250.
Original antialiased shoulder fringe stays exclusively on the cap. A material
extraction mask excludes 16 generated cyan lamp pixels, preventing a duplicate
cyan light on the exposed underlay. The original cap lamps remain intact.
The original source is mostly alpha 253 rather than fully opaque, so overlap
necessarily changes the assembled nearopaque alpha slightly. The measured
neutral composite has maximum alpha delta 5/255 and RGB delta 3/255; mean
RGB delta is 0.211/255 for slate. Retained per-palette metrics, support masks,
source fingerprints, cuts and pivots are in `export-measurements.json`.
Backing covers 98.497% of the selected cap interior at alpha >=128; missing
support lies near the original outer cap silhouette. Runtime pose snapshots
own the visual acceptance of those rims during actual authored motion.

`preview.png` and `preview.mp4` retain the 1920x1080 production-compositor
review: stride, recoil, anti-materiel bracing, launcher overlap, independent
helmet look, all four casing palettes, and a 24px shoulder-unit sample.
Regenerate with `createSnapshots -Psnapshot=specter-layers`; the suite also
retains lossless PNG frames for video encoding. The video repeats its 1.2-second
cycle five times. The backing was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool
using the exact retained prompt, body reference, and annotated cutting guide.
