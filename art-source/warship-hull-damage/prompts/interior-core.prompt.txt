Use case: sketch-to-render, seamless tileable texture. Asset: the gutted interior decks of a starship hull blown open by heavy fire, seen straight down from directly overhead, for a top-down tactical game. Image 1 is the authoritative geometry guide and edit target (1024x1024). Reskin it into a finished painted texture, keeping the layout of every element: the mid-grey field is wrecked deck plating, dented and holed; the dark lines are bulkheads, scorched and partly blown through; the light rectangles are machinery housings, consoles, pumps and power couplings, crushed and blackened; the tan lines are runs of thick conduit and piping, some ruptured and twisted. Soot, scorch blooms, buckled panels, spilled debris and a few small dull ember-orange heat marks. The image must be SEAMLESSLY TILEABLE: whatever leaves the left edge continues on the right edge and whatever leaves the top continues at the bottom, with no border, no frame, no vignette and no lighting gradient across the image. Fully opaque, no transparency. Straight overhead orthographic camera, no perspective, only soft ambient occlusion. Desaturated, medium-dark values overall (it is tinted to each ship's colours at runtime), with clear local value contrast so shapes read at small size. Hand-painted Starsector-like game sprite finish, gritty grounded industrial science fiction. No text, no letters, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no people, no fire or flames, no glowing neon.
