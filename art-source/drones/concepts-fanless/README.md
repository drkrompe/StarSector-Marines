# Fanless drone concepts

Concept pass moving the battle drones off the ducted-fan / rotor look (owner: "too into the 'fan' concept").
Each `*-brief.txt` is the brief as sent to `tools/codex_imagegen.py`; `*-raw.png` is the master and
`*.provenance.json` records the references and their roles (tt-hover-scout for rendering style; vanilla
tempest, hound and wolf sprites for livery and material only). Banned by name in every brief: exposed fans,
fan blades, rotor rings and discs, propellers, ducted props, shrouded lift fans, round ducts, quadcopter arms.

- `a-vectored`: faceted pods on swivel nozzles and slot vents (Tri-Tachyon roles).
- `b-lifting`: flat lifting bodies on a gravitic belly plate (Tri-Tachyon roles).
- `c-gimbal-v2`: the payload in a gimbal yoke on a thruster spine (Tri-Tachyon roles). v1 was refused by the image filter.
- `d-factions-v2`: one gun drone as low-tech, midline and high-tech build it, plus the swarm dart. v1 was refused.

Concept sheets only; sprites for the chosen direction are generated from geometry guides afterwards.
