Concept sheet for a science-fiction strategy video game: the same small hovering robotic DRONE unit, about a metre long, as three different spacefaring factions would build it, plus one small swarm drone. Four drones in a row. Each carries one forward-facing emitter or barrel housing and hovers low over a battlefield map; none uses a fan.
- LOW-TECH: a blunt, heavy, slab-sided armoured box of riveted pressed steel with a stubby barrel housing, held up by two bolted-on downward jet lift pods on outriggers with scorched nozzles and hazard-yellow striping; crude, overbuilt frontier kit; image 2 is its material reference.
- MIDLINE: a practical angular armoured pod in grey and muted blue with a small turret on a short mast, lift from four flush belly thrust grilles shown as slotted panels at the corners, toolbox utility; image 3 is its material reference.
- HIGH-TECH: a smooth flat pearl-white lifting body with a cyan emitter lens on the centreline, gravitic lift with only a thin cyan edge glow, Tri-Tachyon corporate precision; image 4 is its material reference.
- SWARM: a cheap mass-produced half-metre dart from an old abandoned automated security network: gunmetal stamped steel, one small rear thrust nozzle and two tiny side vents, a short barrel at the nose, a red sensor lens, worn hazard-yellow chevrons.
Images 2 to 4 are livery and material references only, never shape or layout.
Strict 90-degree overhead orthographic view, no perspective tilt, lit from the upper left, every drone nose UP (toward the top edge), the four drones evenly spaced in one row across a wide landscape canvas, each drawn large and with a small plain caption beneath it naming its role (LOW-TECH, MIDLINE, HIGH-TECH, SWARM). Plain flat dark-grey background, no ground, scene, shadow slab, border or grid. Rendered as finished top-down game sprites in the manner of the attached approved game sprite: grounded, believable, subtly weathered, crisp and readable when shrunk to 40 pixels, not glossy concept art.
BANNED SHAPES, do not draw any of these anywhere: exposed fans, fan blades, rotor rings, rotor discs, propellers, ducted props, shrouded lift fans, round fan ducts, grilles over circular ducts, quadcopter arms. No drone may be built around a circle. The outline must come from what the drone does (its weapon, sensor or warhead and how it is steered), never from a lift fan.
