This sheet is six independent game parts, each centred in its own 512x512 square (3 columns x 2 rows), exactly where the guide places them, on transparent ground. Top row, three ROTATING parts that spin about the exact centre of their square: (1) a radar dish: a worn steel parabolic dish offset from its hub, a feed arm with a small amber lamp at its tip, a counterweight block, so a rotation is visible; (2) a turbine fan: eight swept steel blades round a hub inside a dark rim ring; (3) a smaller six-blade ventilation fan in a rim ring. Bottom row, three 400x400 interior floor installations that fill their guide squares edge to edge: (4) a command console bank: consoles along three sides with glowing amber and cyan screens round a recessed map table and a chair; (5) racked artillery ordnance: three palletised rows of upright shells in steel racks; (6) a reactor core: a concentric ringed housing with a recessed dark ring, a steel inner ring, a glowing cyan lens centre and four conduit stubs. All dark gunmetal and concrete greys with sparing amber, cyan and olive accents; keep each part within its guide square.
