This sheet is parts of a fortress GATEHOUSE seen from directly above, three panels in the first three quadrants of a 2x2 grid of 768x512 quadrants (the fourth stays empty), each a 2-wide by 3-tall portrait panel exactly where the guide places it. Top-left: a gate-tower roof, a hardened reinforced-concrete cap with chamfered corners and a raised inner cap, narrow dark embrasure slits along the left flank and on both the top and bottom ends, and a small round roof hatch. Top-right: a heavy GATE: the passage between them stays open and transparent, and two thick steel gate leaves stand folded back against the jambs along the top and bottom edges, with hazard striping, big hinge pins and bolts, plus a thin worn threshold sill line across the middle. Bottom-left: the same tower roof DAMAGED: cracked, scorched, one embrasure blown wide. Same materials and registration across panels.
