This sheet is an AMMUNITION MAGAZINE seen from directly above, four panels in a 2x2 grid of 768x512 quadrants, all on exactly the registration of the guide. Top-left: the intact building buried in a thick rounded earth berm (packed dry earth with a few stones and sparse scrub), a concrete crown on top, a central skylight grating through which rows of racked artillery shells can be glimpsed, a concrete headwall at the bottom edge with two heavy steel blast doors with hazard striping, and two round ventilation stacks at the upper corners. Top-right: the same magazine DAMAGED: the berm slumped and cracked, scorch marks, the grating torn open with shells jumbled and spilling, one blast door buckled and ajar. Bottom-left: the same magazine DESTROYED by its own explosion: a wide irregular blast crater with a scorched black interior and churned earth lip, scattered shell casings and bits of ordnance, twisted blast-door plates flung to the corners, transparent open space outside the crater. Bottom-right: a LIGHTS overlay only on transparent: red warning lamps at the door corners and stacks and a faint red glow line under the grating, everything else fully transparent. All states share one registration and materials.
