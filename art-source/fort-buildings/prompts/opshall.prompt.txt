Top-right: the same roof DAMAGED: the skylight smashed with a scorched hole and torn muntins, the cable trunk severed, scorch marks, the upper-left corner bitten away. Bottom-right: a LIGHTS overlay only on transparent: warm amber glow lines in the skylight muntin grid, a small red mast lamp, a thin cyan line along the cable trunk, everything else fully transparent.
