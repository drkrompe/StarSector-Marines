This sheet is the roof of a LOADING BAY seen from directly above, four panels in a 2x2 grid of 768x512 quadrants, all on exactly the registration of the guide (each panel is an 8x3 cell wide rectangle). Top-left: the intact roof of ribbed light steel sheeting (fine parallel ribs running across the short direction), two long recessed skylight strips of dark glass near each long edge, a hazard-striped steel hoist rail running the whole length down the middle with a boxy trolley on it, and a round louvred vent at each end. Top-right: the same roof DAMAGED: ribs peeled back and buckled in one stretch, one skylight smashed, scorch, the hoist rail bent, one corner torn. Bottom-left: the same bay DESTROYED: an open-top wreck, roof sheets caved in and crumpled over a scorched floor, a bent hoist rail, transparent open space. Bottom-right: a LIGHTS overlay only on transparent: amber hazard lamps at the four corners, warm glow in the skylight strips, a small red lamp on the trolley, everything else fully transparent. All states share one registration and materials. Image 2 is a reference for materials and paint style only (existing fort roofs): match its steel and wear, do not copy its layout.
