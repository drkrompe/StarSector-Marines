Asset type: concept sheet for a top-down game: the three machines of a new MIDLINE combat vehicle row, light, medium and heavy, drawn whole (body and locomotion together) in the design language of Starsector's midline warships, the Eagle above all.

The row is ARMOURED CARS on wheels. Each machine is a wheeled armoured fighting vehicle with exposed armoured wheels along its flanks, its bow split into a forked twin prow like the Eagle's with the driver's slit between the forks, and a raised central dome behind them. LIGHT: a four-wheeled scout car, one empty round weapon socket on the dome. MEDIUM: a six-wheeled car, one empty round socket in each prow fork and one on the dome. HEAVY: an eight-wheeled car, large empty round sockets in each fork, a big empty turret ring on the dome, and two square missile mounts on the rear deck.

Every weapon mount is drawn EMPTY: a dark recessed round socket ring or a square hatch, with no gun, barrel, launcher or turret in it. Guns are separate parts added later.

Every machine faces the TOP edge of the sheet. Each machine is symmetric about its own vertical centre line.
