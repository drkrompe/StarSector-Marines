# Talon Two portrait

Original fictional gunship pilot, generated using built-in ImageGen.
The ivory/orange flight helmet, raised visor, microphone, moustache and cockpit
lighting distinguish him from the infantry radio cast.

- Master: [talon-two.master.png](talon-two.master.png)
- Final generation prompt: [talon-two.prompt.txt](talon-two.prompt.txt)
- Runtime: [256px PNG](../../mod/graphics/portraits/talon-two.png)

Export with `art-source/portraits/pack.ps1 -Name talon-two`.
The production subtitle panel displays the portrait at 88px.
Review it beside the infantry with `createSnapshots -Psnapshot=radio-portraits`.
