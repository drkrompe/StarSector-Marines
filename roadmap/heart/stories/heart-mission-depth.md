# Heart mission depth: from brawl to a thought-through battle

Status: ACTIVE. Step 1: candidate beats in `heart-story-beats.md`, awaiting the owner's pick. Seeds: the pooled 2, 3 and 5 on every biome (`story-map-pools.md`).

Written: 2026-10-23

## Why

Tuning the heart to win most seeds made it work as a brawl, not as a mission
worth playing (owner, 2026-10-23: "This is how we make this mission fun and not
just tune it to work as a brawl"). Win rates move with every combat change on
main. The mission needs authored depth before it needs more tuning, and that
work is done in this order. Each step builds on the one before it.

## Order of work

1. **Story beats.** What happens in this battle that a player remembers, beyond
   the bores falling in turn. The beats come first because the map and the
   forces exist to stage them.
2. **Maps with intent, on the controlled seeds.** Hills, valleys, roads and
   satellite structures. A field commander's defence line and logistics put
   where one would really have set them up. The detail that makes the map
   read as thought through, not generated.
3. **The allied forces.** What the militia would really have brought, and
   whether it makes sense for the map and the beats. Room for more
   interesting tools: tanks, close air support, and logistics bases that send
   up vehicles or call in CAS. What the force is made up of.
4. **The allied commander using them.** How the AI commander uses those tools
   intelligently: when to commit armour, call CAS, or feed a lane from a base.

Tuning win rates follows these steps; it does not lead them (owner: no
overtuning yet).

## Constraints

- `heart-nouns.md` stays the model: new nouns (a logistics base, a CAS call)
  amend it rather than live here.
- Owner choices go to the picks page as pictures, relayed through the
  coordinator.
