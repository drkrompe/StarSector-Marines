# Cordon company command

Status: PLANNED

Written: 2026-10-22

Model: `cordon-nouns.md` (Line posture). Depends on `cordon-battle.md`.

## Scope

The Marine commander for both roles, grown from the rescue commander's parts
(`RescueEscortCommand`, `ReadinessGate`, `StrikeDetachment`): the line posture
(Hold, Opportunistic, Push) chosen at the briefing and frozen; the readiness
gate with the Cordon's inputs (line or road held for a window; strength left
covers calling redoubts and people on the move; a capable detachment); relief
of a calling redoubt and the road picket as assignments; strike detachments
at the bore pressing hardest. Beats `strike_committed_N` / `strike_recalled_N`
as Colony Breach's.
