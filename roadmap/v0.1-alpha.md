# v0.1 Alpha release tracker

Status: DRAFT — the force envelope is recorded; mission selection and map scale remain open.

Written: 2026-10-01

This cross-feature tracker points to the existing roadmap stories that must be
accepted or completed before a public v0.1 alpha. The linked feature boards and
stories remain authoritative for implementation detail and their own status.

## Target envelope

- A player can start from a fresh campaign, form and equip a company, reach
  beginner work, launch operations, settle outcomes, recover or replace losses,
  and continue into harder work.
- Marine force: one to twenty campaign squads across the alpha progression.
- Heavy force: two to eight persistent lances, each a squad of up to four
  mechs or vehicles. That is a maximum of thirty-two heavy chassis across the
  company. This tracker reads the target as eight lances total; whether a lance
  may mix chassis types remains open.
- The mission set should emphasize early progression and mid-sized maps. The
  current tier curve does not fit that envelope at its upper end: Reinforced
  recommends seventeen Marine squads and uses a 280×168 large map, while the
  144×80 medium map covers Established and Veteran. Decide whether v0.1 accepts
  the large Reinforced map or needs a new scale curve before balance work.
- The alpha mission list is not fixed here. Do not treat every registered
  mission family or debug surface as a release promise.

## Decisions to resolve

1. Which mission families and named operations are in v0.1, beyond the two
   existing opening jobs?
2. Does the twenty-squad ceiling require the 280×168 Reinforced map, or should
   that force fit the medium-map target through a new curve?
3. Are mech and vehicle lances one shared eight-lance company limit, or two
   independent rosters? Can a single lance mix mech and vehicle chassis?
4. What dependable first-offer route avoids making a new player search multiple
   Independent brokers for the opening work, which is currently offered at
   roughly one in three brokers?
5. Does Retreat remain a no-settlement exit for alpha, or should it settle a
   battle outcome like defeat?

## How to maintain this tracker

Update release status when the linked story boards or the accepted scope change.
Keep detailed plans and acceptance criteria in their owning stories; add a new
feature story before implementation wherever this table says no covering story
exists. A release gate is green only after its acceptance evidence is recorded,
not merely because its implementation story is marked shipped.
