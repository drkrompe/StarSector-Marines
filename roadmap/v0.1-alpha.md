# v0.1 Alpha release tracker

Status: DRAFT — Conquest is the alpha direction; mission scope and roster sizing remain open.

Written: 2026-10-01
Updated: 2026-10-12 — Second Wind bay and machine-shop restoration implemented; live acceptance remains.

This cross-feature tracker points to the existing roadmap stories that must be
accepted or completed before a public v0.1 alpha. The linked feature boards and
stories remain authoritative for implementation detail and their own status.

## Target envelope

- A player can start from a fresh campaign, form and equip a company, reach
  beginner work, launch operations, settle outcomes, recover or replace losses,
  and continue into harder work.
- Marine force: one to twenty campaign squads across the alpha progression.
- Heavy force: two to eight persistent lances, each a squad of up to four
  mechs or vehicles. Mechs and vehicles use separate rosters. The exact cap
  across those rosters remains open, so the 32-chassis upper bound applies only
  if the eight-lance target is shared.
- Conquest is the alpha's primary play mode, toned down for early progression.
  The exact starting mission sequence, forces, and reward pacing still need
  definition.
- Overall map dimensions of 300 or more are acceptable. Treat the playable
  combat area as a separate design boundary; the larger map can provide room
  for ships, fighters, and other scene elements. Balance early fights to the
  player's force rather than requiring the whole map to be combat space.
- Mech and vehicle lances use separate company rosters and control paths because
  their movement and AI differ. The 2–8 lance target still needs a precise cap:
  total across both rosters or per roster.
- Do not treat every registered mission family or debug surface as a release
  promise.

## Company preparation pickup

The sealed mess reopens for 2,500 credits after Relief and grants company infantry
25% faster morale recovery on future deployments from the active original home.
`ship-condition-survey.md` makes Engineering a persistent navigation tab with a selectable room survey
with Castle's condition blurbs and reviewed yard orders. Second Wind's Mech Lab
starts sealed until mission two's Wayward recovery; spare billets and faulty
distribution circuits have paid restoration paths after Relief. Live campaign
acceptance remains open.

`ship-ambient-wear.md` adds persistent scuffs and old repair scars across Second
Wind, with steady room lighting and localized emergency-light faults in damaged
facilities. Existing repairs restore steady lighting. Render evidence is ready;
live visual/performance acceptance remains open.

`machine-shop-restoration.md` extends Second Wind's restoration into clearing,
machinery repair and installed tooling, with visible room changes and concrete
reclamation/fabrication benefits. This optional chain follows the first mission;
implementation and construction evidence are ready, with live acceptance open.

`cerberus-bay-restoration.md` adds Second Wind's reserved damaged second shuttle
bay. Repair after Hold Until Relieved is an optional 8,000-credit planetary yard
order; it preserves the layout and opens an empty berth for paid commissioning.
Implemented with paired deck/UI evidence; live campaign acceptance remains open.


`cerberus-company-start.md` adds the optional canonical founding route: take command
of one Cerberus, fly to a planetary spaceport, pay for the permanent conversion,
and designate the company vessel. A finite prepaid planetary depot covers the
shuttle and squad bills while docked, preserving travel reserves without a freighter
or market-shopping detour. Live start and save acceptance remain open.

`first-mission-preparation.md` is the cohesive opening: choose a company ship
with the complete remaining stores available, commission its first reusable shuttle,
meet Mira and choose her specialty, then found and assign her full squad. Castle's
portrait popovers and real UI highlights lead to Hold Until Relieved through the
existing Jangala contact/course route. `boat-condition-and-repairs.md` follows
with persistent surviving damage and explicit repair bills.

`barracks-turnaround.md` is the active company-flow pickup: survivor consolidation,
reserve/cargo replenishment, post-mission preparation and a replayable walkthrough
from the existing pragmatic, matriarchal company officer. Wounded soldiers keep
their billets. `company-resource-bills.md` follows with consistent Cost, Available
and Short by presentation across company rooms.

The next active pickup is `officer-careers.md`: dossiers, frozen participation
credit for every commander, and three visible passive specialty paths.

## Decisions recorded

- Mara Venn on Jangala is the canonical Independent introduction. Relief unlocks
  her Counterattack; completing both opens repeatable work at other Independent
  brokers under distinct job identities. Each story chapter stays completed;
  defeat permits retry. Mara's debriefs and completed dossier close the arc.
  HQ identifies her office and plots a course. An accessible planetary
  fallback covers campaigns where Jangala is unavailable.

- Alpha content direction includes a complete Wayline arc and a follow-on choice
  between bringing a settlement crew home and preserving its freight route.
  The implemented follow-on in `early-operation-nouns.md` provides a common Junction
  capture battle, then Last Lift escort/boarding or Relay Nine capture/hold, with
  separate recurring contacts. Last Lift preserves rescued and confirmed lost
  people across attempts; retries cover only those still stranded. Junction has
  a finite territorial factory and fitted-boat replay evidence. Last Lift now has
  an escort/boarding factory and save-owned retry ledger. Relay Nine has separate
  militia holds and a finite road counterattack. Campaign
  launch/progression, credit writeback and recurring work are connected; live
  acceptance remains in `broken-supply-route.md`.
- Conquest is the primary alpha mode, toned down to support early progression.
- Maps may exceed 300 dimensions; combat boundaries can cover only part of the
  map, leaving room for ships and fighters.
- Mechs and vehicles have separate rosters and control paths.
- Officerless Marine squads retain basic autonomous leadership through acting
  NCOs; named officers improve group coordination in Conquest. Rank limits
  coordinated group size rather than blocking deployment. See
  `mission-command-nouns.md`.
- Retreat settles through the existing defeat rules, preserving recorded losses
  without adding withdrawal casualties. Ordinary defeat owns rewards, recovery,
  and progression; debug-source operations remain non-persistent. See
  `campaign-nouns.md` and the live pass in `retreat-defeat-settlement.md`.

## How to maintain this tracker

Update release status when the linked story boards or the accepted scope change.
Keep detailed plans and acceptance criteria in their owning stories; add a new
feature story before implementation wherever this table says no covering story
exists. A release gate is green only after its acceptance evidence is recorded,
not merely because its implementation story is marked shipped.
