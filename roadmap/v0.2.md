# v0.2 release tracker

Status: DRAFT — scope is being collected; the first item is the mech lines.

Written: 2026-10-23

Updated: 2026-10-24 — generic contract work and progression, taken out of v0.1.

This is the cross-feature tracker for the release after the v0.1 alpha
(`v0.1-alpha.md`). It points to the roadmap docs and stories that make up v0.2.
Those docs, and the feature boards they sit on, remain the authority for detail
and status. Work belongs here only once the owner has assigned it to v0.2.

## Scope

| Item | Owner doc | State | What it delivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mech lines | `mech-lines.md` | Direction; no story yet | New mech chassis beyond the three stand-ins (Hound, Warden, Bulwark), as lines by tech family and weight class (`tech-families.md`). The low-tech row comes first, for the campaign's Hegemony, Independent and pirate space: low-tech medium and heavy, then low-tech variants. |

### Taken out of v0.1: generic contract work

In v0.1 the only missions the game seeds by planet are the Rav Bloom's;
everything else is story work and the jobs a story seeds (`contracts-nouns.md`,
Where missions come from). The generic contract work below was removed from
the campaign rather than shipped below that bar. **Each returns only with map
and mission planning that meets the Rav Bloom missions' quality bar** (owner,
2026-10-24): its own map intent, allies and story beats, the way the Cordon
and the heart have theirs. The removed code is in Git history.

| Item | Owner doc | State | What it delivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raid and Extraction contracts | `contracts-nouns.md` | Removed from v0.1 | Strike (fought as Raid) and Escort (fought as Extraction) patron work, rebuilt as planned missions. Its offer sources were the standard patron generator (`ContractGenerator`, `ContractOfferTemplate`), threat-intervention offers against learned plots, and paired civil-war participation offers. |
| Planetary Assault and campaign Conquest | `contracts-nouns.md`, `conquest-nouns.md` | Removed from v0.1 | The phased Tier 3 contract (Sabotage, Raid, Assault, Conquest). Until it returns, the campaign reaches Conquest only through the player's own Nexerelin invasion; the debug selector keeps it. |
| Stationing contracts | `contracts-nouns.md` | Offers removed from v0.1 | Garrison and Cadre patron offers, with their stationing incidents, garrison defences and default extraction as planned missions. Postings at the player's own colonies stay in v0.1. |
| Contract board stories | `stories.md` (campaign contracts) | Deferred | `mission-offer-acceptance.md`, `contracts-live-acceptance.md`, `tier-one-stationing-policy.md`, `cadre-incident-failure-reputation.md`, `stationing-event-label-policy.md`, `faction-ground-contract-policy.md`. |
| Progression rework | `progression-nouns.md`, `meta-progression.md` | Deferred; progression is not meaningful in v0.1 | What earns company rank, MRB credibility and patron standing once contracts are gone (Bloom calls and story jobs are the candidates), and what a military warrant asks in place of the retired service term and service calls. |
