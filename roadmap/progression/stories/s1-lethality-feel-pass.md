resolve quickly enough for positioning and cover to matter without making weak

player equipment, then include:

- a living-world alien or swarm encounter, whose pre-pass exchange rate was

- a Surplus Field Rifle against heavy armor, the intentionally harsh bottom

Record only observable failures and their reproduction context. If tuning is
needed, contract a separate bounded story against the standing quality laws;
do not reopen the retired implementation log.
