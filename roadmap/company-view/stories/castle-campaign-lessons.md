## Boundaries

No payout, rank threshold, equipment milestone, battle balance or repair-price
changes. Optional purchases remain optional; depleted companies may still use
ordinary permitted launch minima. Debug fixtures cannot advance campaign lessons.
Persistent tutorial receipts record learning only, never award stock or own
mission progression. First Refit's and Mech Bay's existing transactions own their
completion. Lost assets must not trap a lesson at an impossible order.
