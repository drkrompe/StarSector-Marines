# Boat condition and repairs

Status: PLANNED
Written: 2026-10-11

Preserve surviving boat condition across campaign missions, expose explicit
supplies/metals/machinery repair costs scaled by damage, and introduce the order
through Castle's portrait and click-through lesson after the first damaged return.
Destroyed boats remain full replacement purchases. Repaired boats retain their
identity and fittings. Reconcile tactical servicing with campaign condition so
carrier rearming cannot silently erase the persistent repair bill.

Read `company-view-nouns.md` and `air-nouns.md` before implementation. Requires
once-only condition settlement, legacy healthy defaults, atomic repair spending,
clear airworthiness rules and live return/repair/redeployment acceptance.
