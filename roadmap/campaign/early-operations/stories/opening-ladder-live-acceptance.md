# Opening ladder live acceptance

Status: READY

Written: 2026-08-23

Updated: 2026-08-24 — added acceptance for the relief anchor and bandit depot as legible capability-bearing places.

Read `early-operation-nouns.md` before accepting this story.

## Goal

Verify that the two-rung Independent opening is a readable, winnable green
company experience with the current twelve-marine squad and lift behavior.

## Acceptance

- Relief's local line survives long enough for a slow player transport to
  matter, but cannot win unattended.
- Relief's defensive anchor reads as a communications/logistics place the local
  militia must preserve, not an arbitrary patch of ground. It supplies mission
  purpose without adding a static-defense or reinforcement capability.
- The first briefing clearly accepts one starting transport and one selected
  fire team; counterattack clearly communicates its larger player-lift demand.
- Live results justify the local-militia versus bandit ratio and approach
  pressure without adding regulars, mechs, turrets, fighters, or reinforcements.
- Defeat leaves each rung retryable; victory removes it exactly once and makes
  the counterattack available after relief even if the company is no longer
  green.
- Squad assembly at the landing zone reads as deliberate rather than stalled
  before the finite-force commander pressure begins.
- Counterattack makes the bandit depot visible and names seizure of that depot
  as the reason for the joint advance. Victory reads as securing the place,
  not merely discovering that the final bandit died.
- The two missions introduce preserve/secure objective language consistent with
  `faction-ground-contract-policy.md` without adding objective mandates to the
  already-shipped one-shot ladder before that contract seam exists.

## Out of scope

A third pinned-assault or fabrication-cache recovery variant, new campaign
eligibility brackets, parts/schematic payout, and changes to general
operation-tier policy. A third variant remains uncontracted until this live
pass supplies evidence, as required by `early-operation-nouns.md`.
