# Opening ladder live acceptance

Status: READY

Written: 2026-08-23

Read `early-operation-nouns.md` before accepting this story.

## Goal

Verify that the two-rung Independent opening is a readable, winnable green
company experience with the current twelve-marine squad and lift behavior.

## Acceptance

- Relief's local line survives long enough for a slow player transport to
  matter, but cannot win unattended.
- The first briefing clearly accepts one starting transport and one selected
  fire team; counterattack clearly communicates its larger player-lift demand.
- Live results justify the local-militia versus bandit ratio and approach
  pressure without adding regulars, mechs, turrets, fighters, or reinforcements.
- Defeat leaves each rung retryable; victory removes it exactly once and makes
  the counterattack available after relief even if the company is no longer
  green.
- Squad assembly at the landing zone reads as deliberate rather than stalled
  before the finite-force commander pressure begins.

## Out of scope

A third pinned-assault variant, new campaign eligibility brackets, and changes
to general operation-tier policy.
