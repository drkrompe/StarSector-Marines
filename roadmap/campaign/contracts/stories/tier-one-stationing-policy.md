# Tier-one stationing policy

Status: DRAFT

Written: 2026-08-23

## Goal

Reconcile the ordinary-offer policy with its lowest-rank stationing promise.
Current generation permits Tier 1 Strike only, while the retired model described
a one-month Tier 1 Garrison. Decide deliberately whether Tier 1 can commission
that constrained Garrison work or whether stationing begins at Tier 2.

## Scope

- Choose one durable rank-gating rule for ordinary stationing offers.
- If Tier 1 stationing remains intended, make generation and acceptance expose
  only the defined one-month Garrison commitment.
- If it is not intended, preserve the current policy and ensure all contract
  guidance says stationing begins at Tier 2.
- Cover the selected boundary with focused policy tests.

## Constraints

This does not rebalance retainers, add Tier 1 Cadre, or alter Tier 4/endgame
authority. It changes a rank gate only after the desired player-facing policy is
chosen.

## Acceptance

- Offer generation, direct acceptance validation, and contract guidance agree
  on the selected Tier 1 rule.
- No Tier 1 path grants a work type beyond the selected boundary.
