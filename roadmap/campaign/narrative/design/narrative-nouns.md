# Campaign narrative nouns

Status: ACTIVE

Written: 2026-08-23

Updated: 2026-10-13 — procedural officer reports remain distinct from ordered authored mission correspondence.

## Purpose

Campaign narrative makes contracts legible as a lived patron tapestry without
claiming omniscience. The comms officer narrates procedural contracts through
fact-bound reports. Authored story correspondence may instead present an ordered
exchange among named characters; it does not make procedural reports omniscient.

## Vocabulary

- **Patron register** is the situation a patron archetype gives the officer to
  report; it is distinct from house flavor, rank, and hidden motive.
- **Officer mood** is the company's current frame around that report. It does
  not alter patron facts.
- **Engagement memory** is one immutable, source-unique record of a real
  terminal contract outcome. It is not inferred reputation or a biography.
- **Direct memory** is the current patron's own history with the company.
- **Chronicle reference** is a recent, confirmed public fact naming the current
  patron. **Local echo** is a recent same-market engagement by another house.
- **Remembered target** is a frozen operation location rendered as player-facing
  prose when current economy data can resolve it.

- **Authored dialogue** is immutable, optional mission correspondence whose ordered
  turns retain stable character identities, named speakers and their words. Plain mission
  flavor remains a supported source when no explicit dialogue is supplied.

## Laws

1. Narrative states only persisted, player-available facts. It never invents
   motive, relationship, hidden knowledge, or off-screen consequence.
2. Engagement memories append exactly once from their terminal authority and
   survive source-contract compaction. Invalid and legacy facts fail closed.
3. Briefing context has one relevance order: direct patron history first,
   then a patron-linked confirmed Chronicle fact, then one local echo. A
   returning patron is never displaced by unrelated local color.
4. Memory selection and prose are deterministic. Target identity is frozen
   before rendering, while a missing live name produces neutral prose rather
   than an id or a fabricated place.
5. Archetype text, officer mood, and factual callbacks remain composable axes.
   No axis may smuggle facts owned by another one.
6. Narrative presentation is observation and correspondence only: it does not
   change contracts, reputation, economy, Chronicle history, or moral state.

## Flow and boundaries

Contract and campaign-event authorities freeze qualifying outcomes. Narrative
selects at most one relevant fact for the current briefing, composes the
officer's frame with the patron register, and renders it through the existing
briefing surfaces. The same frozen facts can ground after-action observation
without turning either surface into a political simulator.

Contract previews and briefings project the same authored turns. Stable speaker
colors identify names; dialogue bodies remain white and readable independently
of audio. Recorded turns may change speakers, with portrait, name and role
following the current recording. Authored negotiation prose explains its situation;
commercial choices and their effects remain contract-owned commands.

`contracts-nouns.md` owns commercial terms and terminal outcomes;
`living-world-nouns.md` owns political and Chronicle truth; `themes.md` owns
flavor framing; `moral-compass.md` owns hidden moral facts. Narrative owns only
their truthful, bounded presentation. Longer patron arcs, anomaly events,
captain observations, and archetype evolution require a new explicit story.

The current live work is `narrative-live-acceptance.md`.
