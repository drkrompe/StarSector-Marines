# Personnel live acceptance

Status: PARKED

Written: 2026-08-23

Read `personnel-nouns.md` before resuming this story.

## Purpose

Confirm the shipped personnel lifecycle in an actual campaign presentation.
This is acceptance work, not a license to change personnel policy, balance, or
implementation while testing it.

## Acceptance

- Exercise a normal briefing with an explicit squad selection and confirm that
  a selected shortage, a company shortage, and an intentional empty selection
  produce distinct, truthful guidance without substituting unrelated people.
- Confirm a deployed squad's debrief makes the named outcome and recovery state
  legible without turning employer or debug personnel into campaign marines.
- Observe a captain candidate after eligible derelict salvage: normal loot
  remains intact; accept, decline, defer, and full-roster states are clear; a
  resolved candidate cannot duplicate a captain.
- Confirm captain outlook presentation is diegetic and readable without
  exposing moral dimensions, numerical scores, thresholds, or combat effects.

## Boundaries

Validate the current single-operation settlement rule, not unshipped
per-officer settlement. That work belongs to `c13-the-task-force.md`.
Do not rename squad-bearing persistence fields during acceptance;
`squad-id-terminology-cleanup.md` owns that compatibility-sensitive cleanup.
