# Player backgrounds

Status: DRAFT

Written: 2026-08-23

## Direction

A player background is a curated mechanical and narrative starting-state seed.
It gives the company context at the start of a campaign without becoming a
cosmetic character creator or a permanent replacement for the history the
player makes in play.

A future background may establish starting resources, company relationships,
captain context, or one bounded narrative fact. It must persist its identity so
truthful presentation can refer to that origin later.

## Constraints

- Backgrounds are selected authored options, not free-form faction, banner, or
  motto customisation.
- A starting advantage must name its persistent owner and must not bypass
  contract, personnel, or economy authority.
- Narrative callbacks may use the selected background only as a frozen fact;
  they may not infer continuing motive or reputation from it.
- No background picker or background persistence is implemented yet.
