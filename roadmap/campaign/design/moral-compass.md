# Moral compass

Status: ACTIVE

Written: 2026-08-23

## Purpose

The moral compass is the campaign's hidden record of what the player's
attributable choices establish about their company. It exists to let the world
and the endgame remember a pattern of conduct without turning character into a
score the player can optimise.

## Record and meaning

The record has independent internal dimensions for mercy, integrity,
stewardship, and institutionalism. A source changes only the dimensions that
its persisted facts support. Each applied source is unique and immutable, so
save/load, repeated presentation, later compaction, and delayed consumers
cannot record the same choice twice.

Current sources are attributed civil-war outcomes, civilian-rescue decisions,
and defector-asylum outcomes. Expiry, unsupported battle inference, and
unattributed autonomous political change remain morally neutral. The current
foundation also supports a fact-bound kingmaker testament and captain outlook
reaction without rendering the hidden values numerically.

## Withholding law

The compass is never a meter, threshold dashboard, reward toast, or negotiation
calculator. Diegetic reflection may describe a remembered act or its human
consequence, but it must not reveal the arithmetic behind it. Presentation
cannot append, reinterpret, or repair a moral fact.

## Boundaries and extension

`campaign-event-nouns.md` owns event facts, `living-world-nouns.md` owns
political attribution, and `t3-endgame-nouns.md` owns the testament boundary.
Narrative may render only facts those owners have frozen.

New moral sources require an explicit choice or outcome, a stable source
identity, dimension-specific justification, and an exactly-once recorder path.
Future social reactions remain direction until they name the facts they consume
and the player-facing decision they change.
