# The Corvus Account

Status: ACTIVE — investigation and military-intervention direction; live balance acceptance remains.

Written: 2026-10-13

## Premise, place and cast

Mara Venn follows the Sable Wake pirate crew's sale of Wayline sailing schedules
across Corvus. Castle turns attributable records into company orders. Asharu,
Warion and Garnir are fixed existing destinations; the isolated pirate facilities
are authored local fiction, not the planets' governments or orbital assets.

The final intervention brings the company beside Hegemony naval power. Captain
Irena Kord and the Onslaught HSS Resolute supply its authored character frame;
Mara remains the commercial and human counterpoint. The canonical standing
dialogue model is in `early-operation-nouns.md`; older character-pass wording
in the active story is not a second authority.

`early-operation-nouns.md` owns the canonical shared and standing chapter model,
frozen destinations, progression, intervention and settlement. This folder
collects arc orientation and remaining work. Its design board lists open stories;
temporary implementation documents in its stories folder are folded and deleted
when shipped.

## Entry and chapter sequence

Either closed Broken Supply Route branch opens the arc, including exhausted
failure. Recurring arrangements retain their original sites. Defeat retries the
same chapter; accepted victory advances to the next actual planet. A missing
destination pauses work instead of relocating it.

Recommendations rise from two to three to four full squads. Difficulty also
changes through objective distribution and opposition quality. An owned Hound
can support the company; the story supplies no replacement personnel or chassis.

Bombardment obeys actual company clearance, real structural damage and survivor
pickup. Evidence, withdrawal, fire clearance and completed extraction are
different facts. The final naval action does not change vanilla ownership,
diplomacy, industry or orbital assets.

## Resolution and next lead

A Cold Account closes the pirate investigation. Its aftermath gives Castle and
Mara time to acknowledge the company's actual cost and the military disparity.
Victory plus acknowledgement of that aftermath opens the optional A Working
Ship recovery opportunity on Asharu through Mara's introduction to Tamsin Vale.
