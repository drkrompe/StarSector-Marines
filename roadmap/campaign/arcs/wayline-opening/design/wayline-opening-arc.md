# Wayline: An Open Road

Status: ACTIVE — opening arc direction; implemented chapters retain live acceptance.

Written: 2026-10-13

## Premise, place and cast

The company earns its first commercial relationship by protecting the Wayline
freight cooperative's drivers and reopening their depot. Independent broker
Mara Venn represents the cooperative; Castle speaks for the company and its
actual personnel. Mara's affiliation does not describe Jangala's government.

Her canonical office is Jangala in Corvus. The shared accessible-office policy
provides a planetary fallback when required. HQ course guidance and Marine
operations lead to the same current office and chapter.

`early-operation-nouns.md` owns the canonical standing model, office policy,
finite scenarios, settlement and distinction between chapters and recurring jobs.
This folder collects the arc's orientation and remaining implementation work.
Its design board lists open stories; its stories folder holds temporary work
documents that are folded and deleted when shipped.

## Entry and chapter sequence

Relief is discoverable before squad founding; deployment checks actual personnel
and lift. Only the current unfinished chapter is offered. Older saved opening
victories retain their progress. Defeat leaves the chapter available.

| Chapter | Tactical objective | Accepted victory reward | Delivery state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hold Until Relieved — Relief | Land beside the checkpoint militia and break the finite road assault | 6,000 credits; depot chapter opens | Implemented; linked live acceptance remains |
| Take Back the Depot — Counterattack | Join the depot advance, capture dispatch and defend against two finite road attacks | 9,000 credits; recovered Hound Wayward and first Mech Bay restoration | Implemented; linked live acceptance remains |

The optional A Proper Issue lesson follows Relief: one free equipment package
and ordinary deliberate issue. It does not block Counterattack.
Counterattack's recovery and refitting lesson teaches the owned Hound and its
physical components. `castle-campaign-lessons.md` owns that tutorial work.

Secondhand Wings is an optional interlude after story Counterattack victory.
The separate second-bay repair precedes a 5,000-credit exchange for two surplus
six-seat Aeroshuttles. This is a purchase, not another mandatory chapter.
`company-view-nouns.md` owns the boats and restoration transaction.

## Resolution and next lead

Militia Relief and Depot Clearance are separate repeatable jobs with new
identities; they cannot reopen chapters or repeat story rewards.
