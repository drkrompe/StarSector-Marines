## How to read this script

This is the chronological dialogue register for **Salvage Rights**, **Cold Start**
and **Working Passage**, on Asharu in Corvus. It includes the briefing, field
calls, debrief and conditional return-to-work language. It is also the character
reference for future writing, so a new line should agree with what its speaker
knows at that point.

`early-operation-nouns.md` owns the mission and settlement rules;
`shepherd-recovery-arc.md` owns arc orientation. The runtime uses localized
dialogue, automatic speaker changes, portraits, Skip and Replay. Changed speech
stays text-only until a matching recording is approved. Existing unchanged field
recordings may still play. No recording has been commissioned by this revision.

**PUBLIC** means known to Castle and the captain at that point. **HIDDEN** means
an author-established motive the character need not admit. **TO BE REVEALED**
names the scene where new knowledge becomes available. **UNDECIDED** is a writing
boundary, not a secret with an answer in this document.

The dialogue below is the campaign version. Debug exercises identify themselves
as exercises and award neither payment nor a ship. A retry preserves confirmed
work and losses; it does not replay the first conversation as a fresh bargain.
Field calls are ordered by mission flow, but proximity, contact, casualty and
hold calls can interleave and happen only when their actual conditions occur. They are not a mandatory radio playlist.

## People and their interests

### Tamsin Vale — yardmaster, Basin Reclamation Cooperative

Vale runs a working salvage outfit. She has to find money for parts, negotiate
with buyers, keep crews working and tell families when someone does not come
back. Some of those crews are family. The camp is a livelihood built around a
claim, not a charitable outpost or an Independent government office.

She is comfortable hiring violence to recover that livelihood. Her grievance
does not make every decision virtuous, nor every other scavenger a pirate.
She gives practical information freely when it helps get work done. Questions
about ownership, cargo and payment make her more careful and less expansive.

- **PUBLIC, first meeting:** Basin worked this claim first. Armed rivals drove
  most of its people out. Vale wants the yard and access to the Colossus back;
  Morrow needs inspection before anyone can sensibly commit to repairs.
- **HIDDEN:** she regrets letting news of the hold travel. She cannot prove
  exactly who passed it on. A referral from Mara is enough to arrange a meeting,
  not enough to show strangers the manifest or name a buyer.
- **HIDDEN:** she values both her people and the cooperative's ability to earn.
  Those interests can pull against each other. She wants the company taking
  risks she cannot ask a few work crews to take, but she does not treat those
  crews as disposable or sell them with a hull.
- **TO BE REVEALED, first briefing:** her cousin's crew has remained hidden in
  the Colossus. Their quiet report explains how she knows the yard is occupied.
- **TO BE REVEALED, Cold Start:** Morrow is useful industrial equipment. Vale
  knows how her builders work and has suitable tooling. She is trying to restore
  a productive asset, not clear an embarrassing wreck off her balance sheet.
- **TO BE REVEALED, after Cold Start / Working Passage:** the original paid work
  has not reopened the outer haul route. Vale can settle the agreed fees but
  wants the additional road contract paid after shipment dispatch. This is a
  cash-flow problem, not proof the cargo is worthless or the outfit insolvent.
- **TO BE REVEALED, negotiation:** she will part with Morrow only for a defended,
  handed-over route and a completed departure. She first refuses, then offers a
  temporary loan. The final concession costs her a repaired working tender.
- **UNDECIDED:** the cargo's contents, legality, buyer and monetary value. Do not
  write contraband, rare schematics, a government conspiracy or a secret fleet
  patron as established truth. Vale's defensiveness supports several readings.

**Voice:** practical, territorial, sometimes tired. She uses concrete names for
work and equipment. She does not give a lecture about scavenger society, announce
her private motivations or make every reply a threat. Warmth shows through what
she notices and remembers, not an instant friendship with the company.

### Castle — company operations, speaking for the captain's interests

Castle has the referral, the survey and a proposed job. She starts with much the
same knowledge as the player. She can spot gaps in an offer; she cannot know what
is in a sealed hold, what Vale can afford or whether a damaged ship will fly.
She prepares terms for the captain, who retains the decision to deploy.

- **PUBLIC:** the company needs paid work and must look after its personnel.
  Castle asks where the assignment ends and who supplies parts and skilled labor.
- **HIDDEN:** she wants the company to leave each job more capable, without
  getting trapped financing a client's next problem. She watches for useful
  equipment once she has seen what it can actually do.
- **TO BE REVEALED, first briefing:** Vale will share the permit and survey but
  not the hold's contents. Castle accepts a bounded security job, not an open
  mandate to seize cargo or pursue every rival on the planet.
- **TO BE REVEALED, Cold Start:** construction drones can give a small infantry
  company real defensive staying power. That observation precedes her interest
  in owning Morrow; it is not a secret valuation she brought to the first meeting.
- **TO BE REVEALED, negotiation:** Castle will take a usable ship for additional
  work rather than wait on a shipment the company cannot assess. Her leverage is
  the new work and Vale's blocked route. It is not a false claim that the company
  supplied Basin's parts or personally rebuilt Morrow.

**Voice:** observant, direct, curious without being omniscient. She can push a
client without humiliating them. She knows the Second Wind was recovered from a
wreck; that history supports confidence in operating repaired ships, not a claim
on someone else's property. Casualty lines take precedence over celebratory ones.

### The captain and company

The captain is the player's role, not an additional scripted voice. The company
is a mercenary employer with a payroll, equipment and people at risk. Helping
Basin and insisting on payment are compatible. The script offers opportunities
and bounded commitments; it never declares the player's unspoken morality,
financial comfort or satisfaction with a casualty bill.

### The cousin, Basin crews and Morrow's hands

Vale's cousin is currently unnamed and offscreen. The cousin's crew stayed hidden
inside the Colossus; the opening report is local knowledge from those people.
They are not the simulated inspection party, repair technicians, outer pickets
or a hidden rescue objective. No scene claims the player rescued them, counts
them as casualties or puts them into the player's roster.

Inspection technicians, fitters and militia have different jobs. Losing a post
does not mean everyone there died. Morrow's surviving hands know the ship and
may choose to join the company after handover; the hull agreement cannot sell
their labor. Wages and future upkeep become company responsibilities.

### Mara Venn, the authorities and the other outfit

Mara makes the introduction after the Corvus work. That is a professional
referral, not a guarantee about Vale's cargo, creditworthiness or intentions.
She has no speaking turn in these three chapter conversations.

Basin operates in Hegemony space under a local recovery permit. Vale would rather
complete profitable work than invite official interest. The script does not
establish a particular confiscation law, an imminent patrol, a political alliance
or a hidden deadline. A neutral Independent outfit still has reasons to keep its
affairs out of a government's hands.

The opposition is a particular armed rival salvage outfit. Its abandoned-claim
argument is reported by Vale. Its members are opportunists contesting a rich
site; this does not establish a unified scavenger faction or prove that they
know the manifest either.

## Backstory and reveal order

Three cycles before this arc, unnamed low-orbit fighting brought the Colossus
and accompanying ships down over Asharu. Basin reached this basin first and
built a camp and access road while working the wreck. Recently, a sealed hold
was opened and rumors drew armed competitors to the claim. These are authored
local events, not an identification of a canonical vanilla battle.

Morrow was Basin's tender, brought to the site separately. The recent attack
forced her crew to put her down in the service cradle with damaged controls.
She did not spend three cycles as an unowned prize from the original wreckfall.
Most Basin personnel withdrew; the cousin's crew remained concealed in the
Colossus. Surviving Morrow hands return with the repair work.

| Point in the arc | What Castle and the captain learn | What remains withheld |
|---|---|---|
| Referral and survey | Location, employer, proposed security work | Cargo, private budget, Morrow's condition |
| Salvage Rights briefing | Claim history, hidden cousin's crew, occupied yard, paid inspection boundary | Contents and buyer; no ship entitlement |
| Successful inspection | The tender appears repairable; Basin can supply the rig, parts and fitters | Whether the full repair and departure will succeed |
| Cold Start construction | The builder wing can make useful field defenses | Any entitlement to the hull |
| Cold Start debrief | Repairs are paid for and finished; outer-road work remains | New payment terms have not yet been agreed |
| Working Passage briefing | Deferred-cash proposal, refusal, loan counteroffer, conditional title agreement | Manifest remains outside the contract |
| Physical climb-out and settlement | Morrow and willing crew join the fleet; upkeep is the company's | No later cargo reveal is promised |

## 1. Salvage Rights

### Before — the first meeting

The survey is already available. Vale begins by identifying the place they are
looking at. Castle lets her establish the claim, then tests the missing cargo
information and the limits of the job. No one has offered the company a ship.

**Vale:** Mara said you'd seen the survey. That Colossus against the north wall came down three cycles ago, after a fight in low orbit. Her escorts are scattered across Asharu.

**Castle:** And your people got to this one first?

**Vale:** First into the basin. We built the camp, cut a road in, spent a season just finding ways through her. The other outfits had their own wrecks to pick over. Then word got out about ours.

**Castle:** You opened something worth talking about.

**Vale:** Someone certainly thought so. They came in shooting while most of us were on shift. We pulled out who we could. Now they're stripping our yard and calling it an abandoned claim.

**Castle:** Anyone still inside?

**Vale:** My cousin's crew, tucked away in the Colossus. They've kept a quiet channel open. The new owners are still picking through the stores. Haven't found all our ways in yet.

**Castle:** What was in the hold, Vale? I'd like to know what brought this down on you.

**Vale:** I told one person too many already. You're being paid to get us back to work. There's enough in that contract to leave the contents of my hold out of the conversation.

**Castle:** Your claim's on file with the local office?

**Vale:** Permit and survey. You're welcome to those. I'd prefer to have the yard working before a Hegemony patrol takes an interest in the rest.

**Castle:** Then let's be clear about the work. We're taking back the yard. Where do we stop?

**Vale:** At Morrow's cradle. She's our tender; her crew put her down when the attack hit. Get our technicians in to inspect her. If they can mend her, there's a separate fee for bringing up the repair rig and covering the work.

**Castle:** Your parts and fitters. Our people keep them alive. We're not signing on to chase every scavenger on Asharu.

**Vale:** Agreed. My militia have the road beyond the yard. The Colossus is for our handlers, and my cousin's crew will stay out of your way. Nobody's asking your marines to go digging through her.

**Vale:** Receiving store first, then the workshop. My militia will follow you in. Cover our two technicians at Morrow's southern service access while they inspect her. Watch the south haul road; that's how the other crew has been getting in.

### After — inspection accepted

Payment and an inspection report are now facts. Vale offers the next paid job;
Castle does not treat that offer as automatic company availability.

**Castle:** Payment's through. Your fitters think she's repairable?

**Vale:** Enough to put the rig together. I won't call her a flying ship yet. I'll send the repair-cover contract we discussed.

**Castle:** Send us the route with it. I want to know where your people will be working.

**Vale:** Workshop, then the pump connection. I've got the parts. What I haven't got is enough rifles to keep moving them under fire.

**If no inspection technicians were lost:**

**Castle:** We'll get the company ready. Keep your fitters together until we can cover them.

**If inspection technicians were lost, replace that Castle turn:**

**Castle:** Your inspection crew took losses. Give us time to put our people in order before we take another party in.

**Vale:** I will. The rig will be on the receiving apron. Come to me when you're ready.

### Withdrawal or failed inspection

No victory conversation or fee. The results account records confirmed company
and technician losses. A later attempt provides a new inspection party; it does
not bring those people back. The briefing retains the original contract and
appends its recorded technician-loss readout. The cousin's crew is not included
in that counter.

## 2. Cold Start

### Before — the repair contract

The selected company starts ashore in the secured yard. Vale still owns Morrow.
Castle asks after the concealed crew, then begins to understand the construction
wing as equipment the company might eventually use.

**Vale:** The distribution rig's waiting on the apron. Same terms we discussed: my parts, my fitters, your company covering them. We get it to the workshop, then bring the pump station back on line.

**Castle:** And the people inside the Colossus?

**Vale:** Still on the channel. I've told them to keep their heads down until the yard's working. I don't need another crew crossing open ground tonight.

**Castle:** The inspection mentions a construction wing. I thought Morrow's drones were for cutting ships apart.

**Vale:** They do both, with the right tooling. I've got a road-gun kit laid out. Connect shore power and we'll find out how many builders still answer. The yard lights should come back with it.

**Castle:** A gun watching the approach would give our people room to breathe. Captain, we'll cover the builders while Vale's fitters work inside.

**Vale:** Select Build Vulcan Turret and put it on clear ground within reach of Morrow's east bay. Keep fire off the drones. They're workshop equipment, not armor.

**Vale:** I've only small pickets on the south haul and quarry roads. If they have to come in, cover them back to the inner line. I'd rather lose ground out there than have nobody left to hold it later.

## 3. Working Passage

### Before — a new agreement

The original inspection and repair contracts are complete and paid. The
additional problem is the outer haul route: Basin needs access its militia
cannot maintain. Castle now knows what Morrow's builders can do, but has no
entitlement to them. Vale needs a road; Castle needs reliable payment. Both
have something to lose in this exchange.

**Vale:** The haulers are still waiting. Our people can't keep the outer road clear long enough to get them through.

**Castle:** That's beyond the yard. You said your militia had it.

**Vale:** I thought they could manage. I need your company to open it, with Morrow building positions they can hold after you leave.

**Castle:** We took the yard and covered your fitters. Both jobs are finished.

**Vale:** I know. Send me a price for the road.

**Castle:** A new contract, paid on handover. Construction and repair cover included. Anything you want moved out of the Colossus stays with your handlers.

**Vale:** I can settle it when the shipment goes. I've paid you, bought parts, kept this camp fed. The next money comes from getting those haulers out.

**Castle:** So our pay waits on your buyer. We don't even know what you're selling.

**Vale:** You don't need to. I've paid everything I owe you.

**Castle:** You have. And I'd rather keep it that way. Pay us with Morrow.

**Vale:** No. We've just put her back together. Who do you think keeps the machinery working here?

**Castle:** Her builders could leave you a defended road. We clear the positions, cover the construction and stay until your militia take over.

**Vale:** And I lose the tender I've spent all this getting back. Those repairs were my parts, Castle. My fitters.

**Castle:** I'm not claiming we repaired her. I'm offering you another job for her. You've seen what our company can do.

**Vale:** Use her for this. I'll let you keep her until the shipment clears, then pay you out.

**Castle:** That still leaves us waiting on it. We need something we can put to work, Vale. You've got a yard full of salvage. We've got a payroll.

**Vale:** The defenses stay here. You don't lift off while my militia are still trying to get into position.

**Castle:** They'll have cover while they move up. We hand the positions over before we leave.

**Vale:** And this buys you no part of the Colossus. Nothing in her, nothing that comes out of her.

**Castle:** Morrow, with title. That's the whole offer.

**Vale:** When the road is handed over and she's clear of the basin. Her crew aren't part of a sale. I'll ask who wants to go; you find berths for anyone who does.

**Castle:** Their choice, our payroll. Send the terms over for the Captain.

**Vale:** All right. I'll send them. And Castle? Get her out in one piece. I've had enough of watching ships come down here.

**Vale:** She's holding a hover. Bring her along the road to the eastern yard. Clear the pump bend and use Build Vulcan Turret on ground her builders can reach, then prepare the narrows. Cover the work and wait for my militia to take over. Keep the company with her for the drive test. If the drives give out, break off; we'll recover her. The agreement stands.

### After — ship accepted into the fleet

The accepted departure receipt now authorizes the actual transfer. This is a
practical handover between people who have worked together, not a revelation
that Vale knowingly gave away a wonder-ship. The company has new capabilities
to explore, maintenance to do and willing crew to pay.

**Castle:** Registry confirms it, Captain. Morrow's ours. I'll get an engineering party across before we give her anything else to do.

**Vale:** Have someone check the drone-bay filters before you put her to work. Basin dust gets into everything.

**Castle:** The hands coming with her have berths and pay on our books. We'll have our engineers walk through with them. Less guesswork than we had with Second Wind.

**If no militia or company personnel were lost:**

**Vale:** Good. They'll know which noises to worry about. Safe passage, Castle.

**If company personnel were lost but no militia were lost:**

**Vale:** I know what it cost your people to get her out. I'll leave you to them. Safe passage, Castle.

**If militia were lost, this takes precedence over the other farewell:**

**Vale:** I'd better go. There are families waiting on a call from me. Look after her, Castle.

### Returning after an aborted departure

No ship is awarded, and the completed repair contract is not replayed. Vale
arranges recovery and another mobilization of the same hull. Confirmed losses
remain; replacement militia are other people. The signed payment-in-kind terms
stand, so the full negotiation does not repeat.

**Vale:** We still have a deal, Castle. Morrow needs another refit before we try again; I'll see to that and send fresh holders. Clear the pump bend and narrows, cover the construction, then hand the positions over. Stay with her through the drive test in the eastern yard. When the road's ours and she's clear of the basin, she's yours.

## Continuing the relationship

The cargo is still Basin's business when the arc ends. It does not need a reveal
to justify the deal. Do not add a final wink that confirms illegal goods or a
valuable schematic merely to close a mystery the current arc has not resolved.

Vale's later optional referral through Mara to Rusk on Agreus follows the actual
handover. That is another business lead, not a reward owed by the Hegemony or an
obligation for the company to accept the next contract. Its future dialogue
belongs with that arc's own character and knowledge register.

For a new client relationship, establish the same useful distinctions before
writing the exchange: what the captain was told before the meeting, what the
client stands to gain, what they will not volunteer, what service they are
actually buying, and what new event would justify changing those terms. Let a
question or counteroffer expose that information when it becomes relevant.
