## Premise, place and cast

On Asharu, the Basin Reclamation Cooperative pays the company to reopen its
shipbreaking yard after armed scrappers occupy it. Yardmaster Tamsin Vale
supplies technicians, equipment and holders. Her priority is a shipment found in
the Colossus, whose manifest she keeps with her own handlers. Castle negotiates
the damaged Shepherd Morrow in place of a third cash fee, drawing on the company's
experience rebuilding Second Wind before knowing how useful the repaired tender
will be. Vale requires the company to finish opening the haul road before taking
the ship. Mara introduces them. The shipment's contents and legality remain open;
no new cargo reward or investigation objective follows from her reticence.

Vale's cooperative operates commercially in Hegemony-dominated Corvus. Its
recovery permit is local, and the armed occupiers are a particular salvage outfit.
The cooperative keeps its yard, installations and the broken Colossus depot.
Morrow's surviving hands return to repair their own grounded tender.

All three visits share one authored basin. Salvage Rights and Cold Start take
place at night; Working Passage departs by day. Geography and completed repair
facts connect the visits without restoring lost company people or equipment.

`early-operation-nouns.md` owns canonical shared and standing recovery rules,
physical construction, persistent milestones and fleet transfer. This folder
collects arc orientation and open work. Its design board lists open stories;
temporary work documents in its stories folder are folded and deleted on shipping.

## Entry and chapter sequence

A Cold Account victory and acknowledgement of its aftermath unlock the optional
offer. The earlier rescue/freight choice and Hound survival do not gate it.
The company can replenish before accepting; the opportunity has no hidden expiry.

|---|---|---|---|
| Salvage Rights | Secure store, workshop and cradle; protect the physical inspection party | 18,000 credits and protected recovery claim | Implemented; live acceptance remains |

Cold Start uses the current selected company already assembled ashore, rather
than another insertion flight or a frozen earlier roster. Delivered repair cargo
and restored power persist; unfinished work remains for another mobilization.
Working Passage uses real vulnerable builders and militia taking established
holds. A completed timer alone cannot transfer the ship.

## Resolution and next lead

Only accepted physical departure awards the actual fleet member once. The claim,
grounded construction and completed repairs confer no substitute fleet ownership.
The permanent award receipt survives sale or loss; later support requires a
currently owned eligible working hull and ordinary mission permission.

Actual Morrow handover opens Vale's optional referral through Mara to Baronet
Elian Rusk on Agreus in Arcadia. `a-small-war-arc.md` introduces that next arc.
