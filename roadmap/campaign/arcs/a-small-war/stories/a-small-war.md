## The promise

The company graduates into sponsored paramilitary work. Its people can protect
a working district and force a local settlement; a properly equipped military
formation remains a threat beyond their new suits' protection. The escalation
is a change in responsibility and endurance, not a demand to administer another
twenty squads.

**Baronet Elian Dormer** hires the company on **Agreus**, the existing industrial
moon of Syrinx in **Arcadia**. His house has a secured claim on **Latchkey Yard**
under Ko Combine administration, inadequate security, and a shrinking ability to
pay while **Sera Voss's Greyhook flotilla**
extracts cargo and levies. **Lenna Saye** represents the workers whose knowledge
keeps the yard useful. The company can remove Greyhook's coercion, but taking the
yard also forces a decision about who can work there afterward.

Dormer needs Morrow's builders as much as its escort. Static defenses and allied
holders keep a reopened site safe while the company moves. The first suit
patterns improve the same named infantry; they do not introduce pilots, another
roster, enlarged squads or a second lift-seat cost.

Equipment belongs to each marine. A squad can combine different armor patterns,
weapons and roles, including regular marines alongside powered assault and support
wearers. The arc adds choices to existing squads. Its suggested mixed section is
one example, not a requirement that any squad share one suit or equipment set.

## The warrant

After the first victory, Dormer offers a **civil-security procurement warrant**.
Its readable terms precede acceptance of Limited Warrant:

- The company may recover designated depot equipment and retain copies of the
  specifically listed refurbishment patterns after successful recovery.
- Dormer guarantees the local supplier's release of service documentation,
  calibration tooling and compatible surplus stock. Those suppliers accept his
  bond within his concession's registered procurement terms. The Ko Combine
  retains planetary administration; Dormer's title confers no authority over its
  other holdings or over Hegemony or Tri-Tachyon arsenals.
- The task covers Greyhook's armed occupation and coercion at named sites.
  It grants no planetary police commission, authority to seize civilians, or
  permission for indiscriminate orbital fire.
- Debt enforcement and future yard management require a separate agreement after
  the site is inspected. Accepting this recovery job cannot silently choose it.

The player accepts an identified contract, not an unbounded oath to Dormer. Wording
and acquired cards make the restricted access tangible. There is no revocable
software license, equipment lockout, per-suit inventory or new fitting charge.
Later disagreement can change offered work; already learned cards remain learned.

## 1 — Water Under Seal

The playable first pass uses two existing Recovered Specter wearers among the
regular Berth Four collectors. Replace those two fittings with the distinct Boarding Jack
when its pattern and layered art are validated; do not describe the interim kit
as that unfinished pattern. The completed dossier records Dormer's sponsorship
and opens Limited Warrant without granting equipment.

**Purpose:** establish why this client needs us and show crude powered opponents
before awarding our own. The company can succeed using the regular equipment
that brought it through the Shepherd recovery.

North Weir has a protected western service apron, two sealed switching rooms on
opposite sides of a covered reclamation conduit, and a lower maintenance road.
Its pumps recover water and process coolant inside closed circuits. Tor Edek
coordinates the
repair watch from outside the tactical space. A named repair load and its
technicians travel through the ordinary convoy/escort path; they stop at contact.

1. Relieve the constables at the apron and escort the load to the isolation switch.
2. Clear the switching rooms. Living technicians reconnect only while the actual
   work area is uncontested and protected by company troops.
3. Bring the selected tender up to safe working range and begin one useful road
   gun. A finite Berth Four party approaches along the maintenance road while
   construction is underway. Two crude powered wearers support it; cover,
   flanking and concentrated ordinary fire must
   remain viable answers. No surprise demand for an unowned anti-tank weapon.
4. Protect the builders through completion. A precommitted second element of the
   same attack contests the road after the gun comes online; constables inherit
   the hold. The gun must engage real opposition in the ordinary replay;
   decorative construction does not teach the payoff.

Victory requires delivered repair cargo, completed reconnection, a commissioned
defensive position and resolution of the committed attack, with surviving company
presence. Elapsed time or merely killing the opening guards is insufficient.
Destroyed repair cargo or loss of every deployed technician fails the attempt.
Another attempt has explicitly replacement cargo and personnel; no corpses revive.

## 2 — Limited Warrant

**Purpose:** earn a credible first suit pattern through a bounded recovery job.
The stores hold purchased surplus security hardware, not a cache of untouched
front-line military equipment. Dormer's authority, the supplier's paperwork and
physical recovery all matter.

Use a compact bonded-storage compound at Low Quay's industrial edge: a shuttered
loading court, a records office and a separate calibration cage connected by
service galleries. Greyhook's Drent Haulage crews are stripping cargo for transfer.
There are no workers standing among enemy targets to manufacture a moral trap.

Nadi Orr supplies a signed clinic shortage list and identifies medical consignments
among the diverted loads. Her report establishes the civilian cost of Greyhook's
seizures. Returned supplies belong to their named recipients, separate from the
company's equipment award.

1. Secure the records office and confirm the restricted stock's shipping history.
2. Escort the supplier's technician to the calibration cage and recover the
   identified kit. Disabling a wearer does not automatically produce a blueprint.
3. A finite Drent Haulage rearguard tries to deny the loading exit. Recover the package
   through ordinary survivor egress while the tender and a small constable group
   cover the cleared approach. The sponsor's people cannot seize the prize alone.

Victory requires both authenticated records and the calibration package returned
to the designated egress by surviving company personnel. A destroyed required
package fails the attempt; the next commission explicitly funds replacement
calibration material from the supplier, while the recovered records stay recorded.
It never pays twice or pretends destroyed stock was intact.

The victory delivers the **Tri-Tachyon Specter Section** card: ten marines in
ordinary kit and two recovered Specter breachers. The suits' patched composite,
worn actuators and short breach window explain why a small security contractor may
field them. Castle points to the card and ordinary Armory fitting; no marine
changes equipment automatically.

**Castle, reward:** “We have the service files and the tools to use them. Take a

## 3 — The Yard's Owners

**Purpose:** use the new assault capability and reveal the conflict that force
alone cannot settle. The captain knows Dormer has a secured claim before landing.
The new information is what enforcing his proposed terms would mean in practice.

Latchkey's dispatch house, tooling hall and operator entrance form three adjacent
territorial objectives. Homes and the heating service occupy a separate frontage.
Lenna provides an agreed evacuation route and machine labels from her safe office;
she is not an invulnerable escort actor or an enemy because workers resist new leases.

1. Take dispatch and the freight gate. Constables occupy the first actual gain.
2. Clear the tooling hall while preserving its marked calibration equipment.
   Voss's prize crews use paired entrances and a finite reserve to contest it.
3. Secure the operator entrance so the maintenance party can work without passing
   through hostile positions. The full handover requires all three sites held
   together and a living technician finishing the observed inspection.

Bombardment is unavailable: both the machines and the occupied service district
are part of the job's explicit preservation terms. No invisible shield or orbital
weapon is invented to enforce the restriction. Ordinary fire and cover damage
still apply. Losing the marked required tooling fails the handover; a replacement
inspection/refit is a disclosed new attempt, not a successful pristine-yard report.

The inspection validates a locally rebuilt **Recovered Arbalest** package already
listed in the warrant, and the mission rewards the **Salvage-assisted Section**
card that issues it: eight regular billets, two Specter breachers and two Arbalest
support gunners. The card brings the whole section; nothing has to be learned
first.

During debrief, show the lease-arbitration order under Combine administration,
arrears schedule, proposed new leases and Lenna's counterproposal as attributable
documents. The debt is real. No hidden
ledger absolves everybody, and clearing pirates has not ratified repossession.

**Lenna, correspondence:** “You have opened the gate. Before you hand over the

**Lenna, on the company's history:** “Zoey Venn says you broke a lien for Wayline

## The handover decision

Before Chapter 4, the captain can inspect both written agreements, ask Castle
to compare their consequences, or leave and return. Only the explicit signing
action commits a route. Both retain Dormer's secured title, debt, the same core
equipment rewards and the duty to remove Greyhook's coercive presence.

| Agreement | What the captain undertakes | Concrete benefit | Concrete cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **House Custody** | Escort Dormer's appointed custodians and put access under their office. Association workshops operate through new leases and debt deductions. | Dormer funds a 12,000-credit premium, payable only with Chapter 5 victory; later district escort work comes through his office. | Independent tool access ends. Lenna records the company's role and does not become its worker-side contracting contact. The court terms remain lawful and burdensome. |
| **Yard Compact** | Escort a joint custody party and preserve inspected common-service access under a signed repayment schedule. Dormer appoints security; the association retains shop operation and auditable service allocation. | Lenna becomes the contact for later district escort work; the existing common workshop access continues. | The company forgoes the 12,000-credit premium to fund the transition guarantee. Dormer accepts the agreement but records the company as an independent counterparty rather than his preferred enforcement contractor. Debt and security duties remain. |

There is no hidden virtue score, instantly cured poverty, or perfect compromise.
Dormer accepts the compact because he gets productive collateral and inspected
repayments; Lenna accepts enforceable debt because she retains meaningful access.
Under House Custody, Dormer's managers really can stabilize schedules and investment.
Under the compact, divided administration retains genuine friction. Neither
agreement makes an individual worker universally grateful or hostile.

Waiving the premium fully satisfies the company's contribution to the transition
guarantee. There is no additional debit, ongoing liability or undisclosed loss of
equipment access. Any later house-specific offer needs its own authored terms;
Dormer's recorded assessment alone does not create a hidden access penalty.

The signed route persists through defeat and reload; victory ratifies it. Defeat
does not let the player sign both versions for their rewards. Before signing,
the company may decline further work and retain its completed fees and technology.
There is no simulated offscreen massacre while the player considers a menu.

## 4 — Terms of Protection

**Purpose:** the turn. Make the chosen responsibility a battlefield task, let the
player use the mixed section before the finale, and show Greyhook acting against
itself for the first time. Revisit Latchkey with the secured entrance and
inspection work visible, rather than erasing the previous victory.

**Voss, attributable warning before the operation:** “Reed collected his share
from that yard. Your new agreement leaves him nothing. I've told him to leave it.
Plan for him not listening.” Her warning discloses no exact unseen force or
arrival tick. It establishes that what follows is Reed's choice, not her order.

House Custody brings a named custodial records carrier, Dormer's estate guards and a
utilities crew. The compact brings a named joint-party carrier, the same utilities
crew and Lenna's fitters, who will not leave the tools while they are being
commissioned. Both use ordinary convoy movement, arrivals and vulnerable
personnel. The actual handover happens at the dispatch house; technician work
remains in the tooling hall. The agreement changes the problem, not the amount of
invisible protection:

- **House Custody:** the estate guards hold the Cinder Steps lane on Dormer's terms,
  which frees a company section and leaves the guards' post as the weak point.
- **Yard Compact:** the fitters stay at the benches under fire, so the hall itself
  must be held rather than merely its approaches.

1. Escort the party in and occupy dispatch. No signature is inferred from proximity.
2. Protect the hall while technicians finish commissioning the captured rig tools.
3. Daro Reed's Berth Four arrive to recover their levy records and break the
   association's ability to refuse them. They bring a mortar team that ranges on the
   commissioning work and two crude powered rigs: Greyhook's own rebuilt hardware,
   the across-the-line view of the kit the company is about to wear. A second finite
   party of prize crews takes the Cinder Steps service lane. Public warnings come
   from actual reports or sightings.
4. Use tender-built positions and allied holders to keep one approach covered
   while a company section moves against the other. Both duty areas fit within
   supporting distance; success must not require a larger squad-management burden.
   Counter-battery is a real answer to the mortar; the tube must have a findable
   position and an infantry approach.

Victory requires the actual party delivered, dispatch secured, the calibration
job finished by a living technician and the committed assaults resolved. At least
one authorized custody representative must survive through ratification. Losing
every representative, the required records carrier, or every technician before
the job finishes fails the attempt and requires a new mobilization. Other
individual casualties are recorded without automatically failing the handover.
The agreement is not ratified by the mere end of an attack timer.

The technicians produce the **Boarding Jack** refurbishment dossier from Greyhook's
captured service records and tooling. This reward belongs to the contracted recovery
work and is delivered on either route. It is not payment for endorsing a political
choice or a bonus for killing powered wearers. The person explaining the reward
may change if an actual casualty makes a particular witness unavailable.

**Dormer, in the Chapter 4 debrief:** “I have written to my family. I would rather
not have. They will answer, and they will want to know why they had to.” He
names no ship and no date; Castle does not guess at either.

The recovered levy files carry the lead into Chapter 5: a dispatch order signed
by Mika Drent whose load numbers match Nadi's shortage lists and identify Black
Sluice's transfer schedule.

## 5 — A Small War

**Purpose:** finish a bounded local conflict with the company visibly capable of
holding ground, and let it watch its own pressure split Greyhook. **Black Sluice**,
Greyhook's fuel and dispatch pier on the outer Silt Basin, sustains the yard levies
and moves their prizes offworld. Breaking this foothold matters without defeating
every pirate operating in Arcadia.

Chapter 4's recovered levy files include a Drent Haulage dispatch order signed by
Mika Drent. Its load numbers match Nadi's shortage report and identify Black
Sluice's transfer schedule. Local pilots confirm the service approach. Castle cites
those sources when proposing the operation; he does not infer a hidden pirate base
from the map or announce an unseen force roster.

The map has a causeway customs block, a sheltered fuel-control house and the
dispatch pier. West of the landing apron lies open approach ground, the basin's
flats and rim country, which Dormer's ships fly in across; nothing of the ground fight
is out there, and the company's boats come in over the north edge. Service galleries provide infantry flanks around exposed road
approaches. Fuel storage is an explicit preservation objective; its controls are
a seizure target, not an invitation to destroy the district's usable supply.

1. Seize the customs block. Allied holders occupy it the moment the company holds
   it, before the company advances; there is no construction step and no tender on
   this chapter (owner, 2026-10-16).
2. Take fuel control. Drent Haulage opposes the company along a known transfer
   road while a finite party of prize crews and Berth Four covers dispatch. One
   visible support-gun position punishes an exposed frontal approach but has an
   infantry flank.
3. **The split.** When fuel control falls, Voss orders Black Sluice abandoned on an
   open channel the company can hear. Drent Haulage takes the order: its surviving
   crews withdraw physically along the service road and leave the map, and the
   Greyhook lifts off from her pad south of the ridge. Berth Four refuses. Reed's people hold dispatch
   out of grudge, and the prize crews split between them. The withdrawal is real
   lost strength for Greyhook, not a scripted despawn of untouched troops, and it
   happens only once the company has actually taken fuel control.
4. Capture dispatch and its surface transfer records. Reclaim any lost required
   site, then escort the agreed local custody party to its actual control room.
5. Victory requires all three sites held, the marked required fuel infrastructure
   preserved, and the living custody party's completed handover. Destruction of
   that infrastructure fails the attempt; any later attempt records a disclosed
   replacement/refit under the employer-loss policy below. Untouched pirates
   elsewhere in the system do not block resolution.

**The duel overhead.** When Voss orders the withdrawal, the *Greyhook*, Voss's own
Venture with Voss aboard, lifts alone from her cradle on the pads south of the ridge
and turns to fight: Dormer's ships are faster than she is, so she does not run at once. The pair
Dormer's family has lent from Citadel Arcadia, a Hegemony Enforcer destroyer and Dormer's own Wolf,
flies in across the west edge to stop her. What follows is a slugging match in atmosphere that goes wherever the
fighting takes it, over the basin and the company's fight alike, in full view of it:
plating torn away, hulls crippled and driven down.

The matchup is the drama, and it is not even (owner, 2026-10-19: "We don't need this to
be an even fight. We could have an enforcer and a wolf even"; later, "We're just going
for something that's fun to watch"). The Enforcer is a slow, armoured Hegemony line ship
with an omni shield and longer guns than hers, fitted as the Hegemony fits it (the base
game's Balanced Enforcer: Arbalests and Heavy Mortars, Harpoons and Reapers); the Wolf is
Dormer's hunter (`wolf_hegemony_Rusk`), fast and fragile; the Greyhook is a heavier, slower
broadside ship with a cruiser's plate and pool that turns slowly. The pair fight her as a
pack relay: the sturdier Enforcer holds her guns with its shield on her while the Wolf
darts in on her quarter and breaks off to vent. Her autocannons glance off the
Enforcer's plate, so she goes for the Wolf. Refits are the one thing the mission tunes
(`BlackSluiceDuel.RUSK_REFIT` on the Enforcer, `GREYHOOK_REFIT` on her; the Wolf flies as
fitted, and since the escape so does she): pools and shields, never guns or plate. The owner removed her two Hound (P)
escorts on 2026-10-18 ("we plainly have a bad matchup"), replaced the Wolfs with two
Enforcers the same day, and made the pair an Enforcer and a Wolf the next; the frigate
pads either side of her cradle stand empty. Her fitting carries no wing and a warship over a ground
battle never launches one, so the fight stays hull against hull. She carries no point defence: her two
small hardpoints, fixed forward, hold a Harpoon and a Sabot rack (owner, 2026-10-19, "refit her with
vanilla missile pods in WS 004/007 and drop the PD claim"), so the Wolf's lasers have missiles to shoot.

- The player's fleet stays out of the sky. The company has no air role in the
  duel and cannot order Dormer's ships; they are the family's, not a support power.
- Dormer's ships fight the Greyhook, not Black Sluice's ground garrison. They
  answer no calls for fire and make no ground objective easier by existing. Stray
  fire and falling wreckage are real on the ground in both directions.
- The fight has an ending (owner, 2026-10-19: "We probably need a shorter duel. We
  can add back the escape for the Venture as a story element to give it a
  conclusion"). Voss orders the run 68 to 84 seconds after the first hull fire
  (where in that window is fixed by the moment of her withdrawal order), sooner if
  her hull falls under half or Dormer's ships are both down; that order is the one
  scripted thing. She flies out across the south
  edge, over her own pads, fighting what is in reach, with Dormer's ships shooting at
  her; she gets away or is shot down onto the basin short of the edge, and nothing
  decides which but the ships. Either way Voss lives: away, or walking out of the
  wreck, so her last line stands on both endings. Dormer's ships stay overhead once
  she is gone. The debrief reports what actually happened, and ground victory never
  depends on it.
- The mission does not end until the ground objectives are decided and the
  Greyhook is off the map or her wreck has struck and come to rest (owner: "We
  won't want the mission to end until all objectives are complete and the Venture
  escapes or is destroyed with the crash"). A ground fight that finishes first
  waits for her; only the company pulling out leaves her still up there.
- Two ships, one visit. Dormer's letter after Chapter 4 explains their arrival;
  they stay to the end of the fight they came for, and over the battle after it. Their presence is a warning to the
  Combine and to Agreus, not Hegemony protection of the district.
- Ship-versus-ship fighting is built (`air-nouns.md`, Overhead ships): hull fire
  is held until Voss's withdrawal beat, and none of the duel's hulls fires at the
  ground. No area is declared for the fight (owner, 2026-10-18); every warship is
  kept off the map's edges by the standing edge pressure.
- Nothing warps in or out. Dormer's edge is the west, the side the company landed
  on and the far side of the basin, which they fly in across already under way;
  the Greyhook lifts from her pad and leaves, if she leaves, across the south edge
  and no other.

Sera Voss coordinates through transmissions from outside the battle. No invulnerable
boss avatar or guaranteed escape is needed: her run for the edge can fail, and she
walks out of the wreck when it does. Her last line on the channel tells the
company she is alive and what she has lost: “Keep the pier. I've lost worse
berths.” The confirmed result is loss of this surface facility and its organized
levy route. The debrief does not invent her death, the destruction of her fleet or
permanent safety for every settlement.

The ending names the selected agreement, confirmed losses and delivered rewards.
The company receives the fee and any earned premium and a closed story dossier.
The once-only settlement sets `AgreusArcProgress.smallWarSettled`, which sends the
Hegemony auxiliary warrant's offer (`progression-nouns.md`, Kit and armory). The
warrant comes because Dormer's family, having lent the destroyers, has noticed the
company; the more demanding procurement contact is the Hegemony itself.

**Castle, closing assessment:** “Dormer is asking what it would take to keep us here.
He wasn't asking that when we arrived.” Loss-aware surrounding text acknowledges
the actual company cost before describing the invitation. The arc ends grey on
purpose (`a-small-war-arc.md`, Story spine): the company is now the private order
that a state leans on, and a later arc can ask what that costs.

The Boarding Jack is a rebuilt loading/boarding frame: compact pressure helmet,

Boarding Jack values are calibration hypotheses. Verify the within-class tier

with disciplined fire and appropriate anti-armor equipment should overmatch these
rigs in exposed combat. Good terrain and supporting tactics can still matter;

**What the player learns is a card.** A loadout card is the technology: the
knowledge and the licence to build and outfit one particular twelve-person
section. There is no learning a suit. Outfitting all twelve marines in powered
armour is simply not something an ordinary contractor in this sector can do
without a licence that says so, and the company does not have one yet.

The Agreus rewards are section cards built around two recovered suits a tier below
their military patterns: the Tri-Tachyon Specter Section (two powered breachers
among ten regular marines) and the Salvage-assisted Section (two breachers and two
support gunners among eight). A new pirate suit follows the same shape. The
Boarding Jack arrives inside its own light section card, and its complete pattern
and presentation must be declared before the card can be awarded.

Each award delivers its validated loadout card through ordinary cargo learning.
Learning is explicit and permanent; fitting is an explicit free Armory transaction.
Already learned or already-carried cards satisfy the award without creating a
duplicate or resetting the chapter. Award entitlement is recorded once independently
of later learning. A deliberately discarded or sold card is not regranted by
reopening a debrief. Spec inspection and Fit Squad advice remain available without
granting another card or silently changing named kit.

**A loadout card prescribes the whole squad.** The player chooses one weapon card
and one armor card per squad of twelve, and each card fixes what every billet
receives. The player never picks the mix inside a card, and a card never borrows
kit from another. Mixed equipment therefore means a card whose billets differ.

**The arc's kit ceiling.** Agreus lets the player taste powered fighting, not
wield it. Every armor card the arc awards or depends on is a section card with at
most four powered billets in twelve, worn as recovered tier-III rebuilds.
All-suit cards, which prescribe twelve powered billets, stay behind the Restricted
tier and the Hegemony auxiliary warrant that this arc's ending sends. If an
all-suit card is reachable earlier through another source, that is a gap in the shared
acquisition gate, not a feature of this arc. Teach why regular scouts, riflemen
and medics remain useful alongside suit wearers. Boarding Jack can enter the
assault pool after Chapter 4, so the preview must show each marine's actual
resolved kit.
