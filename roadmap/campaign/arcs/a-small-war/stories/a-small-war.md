## The promise

The company graduates into sponsored paramilitary work. Its people can protect
a working district and force a local settlement; a properly equipped military
formation remains a threat beyond their new suits' protection. The escalation
is a change in responsibility and endurance, not a demand to administer another
twenty squads.

**Baronet Elian Rusk** hires the company on **Agreus**, the existing industrial
moon of Syrinx in **Arcadia**. His house has a secured claim on **Latchkey Yard**
under Ko Combine administration, inadequate security, and a shrinking ability to
pay while **Sera Voss's Greyhook flotilla**
extracts cargo and levies. **Lenna Saye** represents the workers whose knowledge
keeps the yard useful. The company can remove Greyhook's coercion, but taking the
yard also forces a decision about who can work there afterward.

Rusk needs Morrow's builders as much as its escort. Static defenses and allied
holders keep a reopened site safe while the company moves. The first suit
patterns improve the same named infantry; they do not introduce pilots, another
roster, enlarged squads or a second lift-seat cost.

Equipment belongs to each marine. A squad can combine different armor patterns,
weapons and roles, including regular marines alongside powered assault and support
wearers. The arc adds choices to existing squads. Its suggested mixed section is
one example, not a requirement that any squad share one suit or equipment set.

## The warrant

After the first victory, Rusk offers a **civil-security procurement warrant**.
Its readable terms precede acceptance of Limited Warrant:

- The company may recover designated depot equipment and retain copies of the
  specifically listed refurbishment patterns after successful recovery.
- Rusk guarantees the local supplier's release of service documentation,
  calibration tooling and compatible surplus stock. Those suppliers accept his
  bond within his concession's registered procurement terms. The Ko Combine
  retains planetary administration; Rusk's title confers no authority over its
  other holdings or over Hegemony or Tri-Tachyon arsenals.
- The task covers Greyhook's armed occupation and coercion at named sites.
  It grants no planetary police commission, authority to seize civilians, or
  permission for indiscriminate orbital fire.
- Debt enforcement and future yard management require a separate agreement after
  the site is inspected. Accepting this recovery job cannot silently choose it.

The player accepts an identified contract, not an unbounded oath to Rusk. Wording
and acquired cards make the restricted access tangible. There is no revocable
software license, equipment lockout, per-suit inventory or new fitting charge.
Later disagreement can change offered work; already learned patterns remain learned.

## 1 — Water Under Seal

The playable first pass uses two existing Recovered Specter wearers among the
regular Salt Knives. Replace those two fittings with the distinct Boarding Jack
when its pattern and layered art are validated; do not describe the interim kit
as that unfinished pattern. The completed dossier records Rusk's sponsorship
and opens Limited Warrant without granting equipment.

**Purpose:** establish why this client needs us and show crude powered opponents
before awarding our own. The company can succeed using the regular equipment
that brought it through the Shepherd recovery.

North Weir has a protected western service apron, two sealed switching rooms on
opposite sides of a covered reclamation conduit, and a lower maintenance road.
Its pumps recover water and process coolant inside closed circuits. Tor Edek
coordinates the
repair watch from outside the tactical space. A named repair load and its
technicians travel through the ordinary convoy/escort path; they stop at contact.

1. Relieve the constables at the apron and escort the load to the isolation switch.
2. Clear the switching rooms. Living technicians reconnect only while the actual
   work area is uncontested and protected by company troops.
3. Bring the selected tender up to safe working range and begin one useful road
   gun. A finite Salt Knife party approaches along the maintenance road while
   construction is underway. Two crude powered wearers support it; cover,
   flanking and concentrated ordinary fire must
   remain viable answers. No surprise demand for an unowned anti-tank weapon.
4. Protect the builders through completion. A precommitted second element of the
   same attack contests the road after the gun comes online; constables inherit
   the hold. The gun must engage real opposition in the ordinary replay;
   decorative construction does not teach the payoff.

Victory requires delivered repair cargo, completed reconnection, a commissioned
defensive position and resolution of the committed attack, with surviving company
presence. Elapsed time or merely killing the opening guards is insufficient.
Destroyed repair cargo or loss of every deployed technician fails the attempt.
Another attempt has explicitly replacement cargo and personnel; no corpses revive.

**Castle briefing:** “Rusk pays for the weir to work when we leave. Edek's crew
will handle the switches. We clear the rooms, cover their load, and give the
constables a position they can hold.”

**Rusk, victory correspondence:** “Your invoice will be honored. There is also
equipment in the bonded stores that your people could put to better use than mine.
I can authorize its release. First we have to recover the records that make it useful.”

## 2 — Limited Warrant

**Purpose:** earn a credible first suit pattern through a bounded recovery job.
The stores hold purchased surplus security hardware, not a cache of untouched
front-line military equipment. Rusk's authority, the supplier's paperwork and
physical recovery all matter.

Use a compact bonded-storage compound at Low Quay's industrial edge: a shuttered
loading court, a records office and a separate calibration cage connected by
service galleries. Greyhook's Cask Runners are stripping cargo for transfer.
There are no workers standing among enemy targets to manufacture a moral trap.

Nadi Orr supplies a signed clinic shortage list and identifies medical consignments
among the diverted loads. Her report establishes the civilian cost of Greyhook's
seizures. Returned supplies belong to their named recipients, separate from the
company's equipment award.

1. Secure the records office and confirm the restricted stock's shipping history.
2. Escort the supplier's technician to the calibration cage and recover the
   identified kit. Disabling a wearer does not automatically produce a blueprint.
3. A finite Runner rearguard tries to deny the loading exit. Recover the package
   through ordinary survivor egress while the tender and a small constable group
   cover the cleared approach. The sponsor's people cannot seize the prize alone.

Victory requires both authenticated records and the calibration package returned
to the designated egress by surviving company personnel. A destroyed required
package fails the attempt; the next commission explicitly funds replacement
calibration material from the supplier, while the recovered records stay recorded.
It never pays twice or pretends destroyed stock was intact.

The victory delivers **Recovered Specter** technology. Its patched composite,
worn actuators and short breach window explain why this is suitable for a small
security contractor. Castle points to the card and ordinary Armory fitting; no
marine changes equipment automatically.

**Rusk, signed warrant:** “The schedule names the patterns. The guarantee is mine.
If a supplier offers you anything beyond it, you are negotiating with them.”

**Castle, reward:** “We have the service files and the tools to use them. Take a
look at the fitting before the next drop. Better protection still needs someone
watching the flank.”

## 3 — The Yard's Owners

**Purpose:** use the new assault capability and reveal the conflict that force
alone cannot settle. The captain knows Rusk has a secured claim before landing.
The new information is what enforcing his proposed terms would mean in practice.

Latchkey's dispatch house, tooling hall and operator entrance form three adjacent
territorial objectives. Homes and the heating service occupy a separate frontage.
Lenna provides an agreed evacuation route and machine labels from her safe office;
she is not an invulnerable escort actor or an enemy because workers resist new leases.

1. Take dispatch and the freight gate. Constables occupy the first actual gain.
2. Clear the tooling hall while preserving its marked calibration equipment.
   Hookhand boarders use paired entrances and a finite reserve to contest it.
3. Secure the operator entrance so the maintenance party can work without passing
   through hostile positions. The full handover requires all three sites held
   together and a living technician finishing the observed inspection.

Bombardment is unavailable: both the machines and the occupied service district
are part of the job's explicit preservation terms. No invisible shield or orbital
weapon is invented to enforce the restriction. Ordinary fire and cover damage
still apply. Losing the marked required tooling fails the handover; a replacement
inspection/refit is a disclosed new attempt, not a successful pristine-yard report.

The inspection validates a locally rebuilt **Recovered Arbalest** package already
listed in the warrant. The mission rewards its card. After both recovered roles
are learned, Castle recommends **Salvage-assisted Section**: eight regular
billets, two powered assault billets and two powered support billets.

During debrief, show the lease-arbitration order under Combine administration,
arrears schedule, proposed new leases and Lenna's counterproposal as attributable
documents. The debt is real. No hidden
ledger absolves everybody, and clearing pirates has not ratified repossession.

**Lenna, correspondence:** “You have opened the gate. Before you hand over the
keys, read who is allowed through it tomorrow. Those benches repair the weir's
pumps as well as Rusk's cargo gear.”

## The handover decision

Before Chapter 4, the captain can inspect both written agreements, ask Castle
to compare their consequences, or leave and return. Only the explicit signing
action commits a route. Both retain Rusk's secured title, debt, the same core
equipment rewards and the duty to remove Greyhook's coercive presence.

| Agreement | What the captain undertakes | Concrete benefit | Concrete cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **House Custody** | Escort Rusk's appointed custodians and put access under their office. Association workshops operate through new leases and debt deductions. | Rusk funds a 12,000-credit premium, payable only with Chapter 5 victory; later district escort work comes through his office. | Independent tool access ends. Lenna records the company's role and does not become its worker-side contracting contact. The court terms remain lawful and burdensome. |
| **Yard Compact** | Escort a joint custody party and preserve inspected common-service access under a signed repayment schedule. Rusk appoints security; the association retains shop operation and auditable service allocation. | Lenna becomes the contact for later district escort work; the existing common workshop access continues. | The company forgoes the 12,000-credit premium to fund the transition guarantee. Rusk accepts the agreement but records the company as an independent counterparty rather than his preferred enforcement contractor. Debt and security duties remain. |

There is no hidden virtue score, instantly cured poverty, or perfect compromise.
Rusk accepts the compact because he gets productive collateral and inspected
repayments; Lenna accepts enforceable debt because she retains meaningful access.
Under House Custody, Rusk's managers really can stabilize schedules and investment.
Under the compact, divided administration retains genuine friction. Neither
agreement makes an individual worker universally grateful or hostile.

Waiving the premium fully satisfies the company's contribution to the transition
guarantee. There is no additional debit, ongoing liability or undisclosed loss of
equipment access. Any later house-specific offer needs its own authored terms;
Rusk's recorded assessment alone does not create a hidden access penalty.

The signed route persists through defeat and reload; victory ratifies it. Defeat
does not let the player sign both versions for their rewards. Before signing,
the company may decline further work and retain its completed fees and technology.
There is no simulated offscreen massacre while the player considers a menu.

## 4 — Terms of Protection

**Purpose:** make the chosen responsibility a battlefield task and let the player
use the mixed section before the finale. Revisit Latchkey with the secured
entrance and inspection work visible, rather than erasing the previous victory.

House Custody brings a named custodial records carrier and a utilities crew.
The compact brings a named joint-party carrier and the same utilities crew.
Both use ordinary convoy movement, arrivals and vulnerable personnel. The actual
handover happens at the dispatch house; technician work remains in the tooling hall.
The participants and account differ, not the amount of invisible protection.

1. Escort the party in and occupy dispatch. No signature is inferred from proximity.
2. Protect the hall while technicians finish commissioning the captured rig tools.
3. Daro Pell's Salt Knives attempt to recover their levy records and destroy the
   association's ability to refuse them. A second finite Hookhand group takes the
   Cinder Steps service lane; public warnings come from actual reports or sightings.
4. Use tender-built positions and allied holders to keep one approach covered
   while a company section moves against the other. Both duty areas fit within
   supporting distance; success must not require a larger squad-management burden.

Victory requires the actual party delivered, dispatch secured, the calibration
job finished by a living technician and the committed assaults resolved. At least
one authorized custody representative must survive through ratification. Losing
every representative, the required records carrier, or every technician before
the job finishes fails the attempt and requires a new mobilization. Other
individual casualties are recorded without automatically failing the handover.
The agreement is not ratified by the mere end of an attack timer.

The technicians produce the **Boarding Jack** refurbishment dossier from Greyhook's
captured service records and tooling. This reward belongs to the contracted recovery
work and is delivered on either route. It is not payment for endorsing a political
choice or a bonus for killing powered wearers. The person explaining the reward
may change if an actual casualty makes a particular witness unavailable.

**Voss, attributable demand before the operation:** “Pell collected his share
from that yard. Your new agreement leaves him nothing. You should plan for him
to come and discuss it.” Her threat discloses no exact unseen force or arrival tick.

## 5 — A Small War

**Purpose:** finish a bounded local conflict with the company visibly capable of
holding ground. **Black Sluice**, Greyhook's fuel and dispatch pier on the outer
Silt Basin, sustains the yard levies and transports their prizes offworld.
Breaking this foothold matters without defeating every pirate operating in Arcadia.

Chapter 4's recovered levy files include a Runner dispatch order signed by Mika
Drent. Its load numbers match Nadi's shortage report and identify Black Sluice's
transfer schedule. Local pilots confirm the service approach. Castle cites those
sources when proposing the operation; he does not infer a hidden pirate base
from the map or announce an unseen force roster.

The map has a causeway customs block, a sheltered fuel-control house and the
dispatch pier. Service galleries provide infantry flanks around exposed road
approaches. Fuel storage is an explicit preservation objective; its controls are
a seizure target, not an invitation to destroy the district's usable supply.

1. Seize the customs block and establish a tender-built rear position. Allied
   holders occupy the cleared building before the company advances.
2. Take fuel control. The Cask Runners oppose the company along a known transfer
   road while a finite Hookhand reserve covers dispatch. One visible support-gun
   position punishes an exposed frontal approach but has an infantry flank.
3. Capture dispatch and its surface transfer records. Reclaim any lost required
   site, then escort the agreed local custody party to its actual control room.
4. Victory requires all three sites held, the marked required fuel infrastructure
   preserved, and the living custody party's completed handover. Destruction of
   that infrastructure fails the attempt; any later attempt records a disclosed
   replacement/refit under the employer-loss policy below. Untouched pirates
   elsewhere in the system do not block resolution.

Sera Voss coordinates through transmissions from outside the battle. No invulnerable
boss avatar or mandatory escape is needed. The confirmed result is loss of this
surface facility and its organized levy route. The debrief does not invent her
death, the destruction of her fleet or permanent safety for every settlement.

The ending names the selected agreement, confirmed losses and delivered rewards.
The company receives the fee and any earned premium, a closed story dossier and
an introduction to a more demanding procurement contact. That contact is a lead
for a later authored arc, not an unimplemented shop or a tier-IV unlock button.

**Castle, closing assessment:** “Rusk is asking what it would take to keep us here.
He wasn't asking that when we arrived.” Loss-aware surrounding text acknowledges
the actual company cost before describing the invitation.

## Force, support and failure contract

Target an authored **Established** operation profile throughout, with opposition
quality and objective duties increasing within it. Rusk's minor house rank does
not imply every chapter is a generated Tier-1 patron job. State authored tier,
recommendation and hard launch minimum separately on each offer.

Initial acceptance floor: two selected squads with at least four ready named
marines each, an eligible fitted insertion manifest and actual tender support.
Recommendations above assume full twelve-person squads; the minimum is not a
claim that eight people reliably win. All selected squads may enter through
ordinary repeated trips; Raid-shaped Chapter 2 explicitly has unrestricted
presence rather than inheriting the generic covert two-squad limit. More player
lift never expands the enemy pool to match it.

Rusk contracts a bounded employer heavy-lift sortie for an owned active mech lance
on Chapters 3-5; an empty bay gives no walker. Briefings name the supplied transport.
No Hound survival is assumed and baseline acceptance must win without a mech.
No capital gun run or fighter fleet is supplied. Construction uses the actual
selected tender, finite vulnerable builders and existing placement/range rules.
Allied constables take stable, separate hold duties only after real company gains.

Citadel Arcadia remains a Hegemony logistics and military presence. Greyhook's
local extortion and cargo diversion exploit scattered contractor yards and
contested access; they do not establish control of the system. Rusk is paying
for precise concession security and a working handover. His warrant does not
command a Hegemony fleet, and this arc neither removes its patrols nor promises
that civilian transport is safe. Broader authorities and markets keep their
ordinary campaign behavior.

Each battle authors its finite guards and reserve groups before launch, naming
their ingress and commitment condition. Normal AI owns combat after arrival.
Absent/delayed-player controls must not win through employer troops alone.
Exact headcounts, map dimensions and timings are encounter-design work; they must
be verified before any runtime offer claims the recommendations are adequate.

Retreat uses ordinary defeat settlement. Confirmed people, cargo and machinery
losses persist by identity. Completed inspection/recovery milestones may persist
where a chapter explicitly names them; unfinished work does not earn completion.
Replacement employer personnel and loads are disclosed new mobilizations.
Required economic assets can be replaced/refitted between attempts by the employer,
with correspondence recording the loss; never silently restore them in the same
battle. No phase restores dead company marines, lost boats or lost tenders.

Capture, physical delivery, safe inspection and handover are different facts.
An outcome must freeze the facts required by that chapter before the once-only
settlement of fee, card entitlement, personnel, assets, agreement and next offer.
Stale, duplicate or foreign outcomes cannot perform any of those writes. A saved
choice survives retries; opening a briefing, preview or course plot commits nothing.

## Afterward and campaign pressure

After victory, **District Escort** is a separate repeatable authored escort job
through Rusk's office under House Custody or Lenna's association under the compact. Initial fee:
24,000 credits for a new finite two-squad escort job. It uses new cargo, people and
attempt identities, awards no named story equipment and cannot replay ratification
or pay another premium. Ordinary patron access, if separately offered by Rusk,
still obeys its normal rules; this story grants no automatic house promotion,
MRB tier, generic stationing contract or vanilla faction commission.

In `meta-progression.md` terms, the arc produces Capability (three patterns) and
local Standing (a recorded sponsor/yard relationship), gates on actual Shepherd
recovery and operational Reach, and targets the **Acknowledged** stage. Its
counter-pressure is committing travel and company recovery to a client outside Corvus,
choosing which local relationship to disappoint, and the compact's foregone
premium. It adds no new abstract pressure currency or equipment upkeep system.
Additional general MRB awards require an explicit existing-authority decision,
not a line of congratulatory dialogue that quietly changes reputation.

The ending changes this authored local agreement and available work. It does not
flip Agreus's vanilla faction, replace Ko Combine administration, rewrite industry
output, seize the player's colonies, destroy a vanilla pirate base or grant
sector-wide diplomatic standing.
Those would be separate campaign mechanics with their own consequences.
