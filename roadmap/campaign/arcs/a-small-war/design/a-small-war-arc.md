## Premise, place and cast

Baronet Elian Rusk hires the company to restore the mod-authored Latchkey
District on existing Agreus, the cold airless industrial moon in Arcadia.
His local concession sits beneath Ko Combine administration. Sera Voss's
Greyhook flotilla coerces cargo and yard access; Lenna Saye represents the
workers whose technical knowledge keeps the yard useful.

Vale's practical reference and Mara's introduction bring the company beyond
Corvus. The company earns local responsibility, tender-supported holds and
recovered power-suit patterns for individual marines within its existing squads.
`agreus-latchkey.md` owns the enduring setting, cast and canon/authored boundary.

`early-operation-nouns.md` owns canonical shared and implemented standing rules.
`a-small-war.md` owns the remaining chapters, agreements, rewards and acceptance.
This folder keeps those plans alongside orientation. Its design board lists open
stories; temporary work documents in its stories folder are folded and deleted
when shipped.

## Entry and chapter sequence

Working Passage's actual Morrow handover receipt gates the optional referral.
Purchasing a Shepherd, securing a claim or finishing Cold Start does not create
that history. The receipt survives later sale or loss; deployment separately
requires an owned operational Shepherd and selected Ship on Station support.
Missing or incompatible Agreus access pauses the work without replacing a world.

to retain inspected common access and Lenna's contracting relationship.

chosen contact. The proposed later procurement introduction is a lead requiring

are not delivered by this arc's first three chapters or by this document.
