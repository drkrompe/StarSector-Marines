# House disposition

Status: DRAFT

Written: 2026-08-23

## Direction

The current political field gives houses material ambition, but not yet a
distinctive disposition. A future **disposition** should make houses legible
people rather than interchangeable optimisers: it may influence whether they
act, which rival they choose, their appetite for risk, or their loyalty within
a hierarchy.

Disposition is not currently a house authority, a Chronicle fact, or a
player-facing stat. The existing ambitions in `living-world-nouns.md` remain
the whole implemented decision surface.

## Player knowledge

Any future patron knowledge must be earned through observed conduct and must
change a real player decision. A dossier must not expose a complete numerical
trait grid or convert unimplemented flavour into false strategic information.
Narrative voice belongs to `narrative-nouns.md`; the political system may only
reveal knowledge that its own decisions have made meaningful.

## Constraints for a future story

- Choose a small, deterministic disposition vocabulary and establish which
  political decisions it actually changes before exposing it.
- Keep a house's actual disposition separate from what the player has learned.
- Do not activate relationship interactions or hierarchy loyalty merely because
  the current registered seam exists; their source, cadence, and consequences
  require a separate, bounded contract.
- Preserve the decisive-player principle: disposition can shape background
  pressure, but cannot make autonomous activity erase attributable player work.
