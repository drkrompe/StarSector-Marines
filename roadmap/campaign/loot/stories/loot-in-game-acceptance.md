# Campaign loot in-game acceptance

Status: READY

Written: 2026-08-23

Read `loot-nouns.md` before accepting this story.

## Goal

Verify one real victorious contract mission exercises the authored recovery
decision without a reroll, double claim, cargo-bucket mistake, or misleading
presentation.

## Acceptance

- Results shows the frozen entitlement, recovery modifier when present, stack
  count, and claim budget; reopening the picker preserves the same manifest and
  selection.
- Commodity and weapon cards fit and remain legible at the live dialog size,
  including long modded names; selected and budget-blocked states are clear.
- A selected mix that overflows cargo, fuel, and personnel shows carried versus
  fenced quantities before confirmation; confirmation applies the displayed
  cargo and discounted credits exactly once.
- A `SALVAGE_EXPERT` captain and Salvage Rig/Gantry fleet show their frozen
  recovery effects. An eligible AI-linked strike can surface a core that flows
  through selection and settlement; an ordinary target cannot.
- Forfeit closes the same recovery flow without moving cargo or credits.

## Out of scope

Blueprint recovery, a new tech-recovery trait, and changes to contract
entitlement policy.
