# Last Testament live acceptance

Status: READY

Written: 2026-08-23

## Outcome

Confirm that the shipped kingmaker epilogue is reachable and legible in a
running campaign without exposing its hidden moral model or replaying a
consequence.

## Scope

Use the development campaign panel's kingmaker-testament setup in a running
campaign, then open the Last Testament intel. Inspect the first delivery,
reopen it, and reload when practical. Treat the debug setup as reachability
only: it deliberately does not invoke the irreversible market or diplomacy
ports.

Do not retune civil-war thresholds, edit testimony prose, add rewards, or make
the hidden moral values visible as part of this pass.

## Acceptance

- Before a qualifying snapshot exists, Last Testament intel is absent or
  hidden; the debug setup creates one valid reachable correspondence.
- The rendered entry names the political act and uses remembered deeds plus a
  concise judgment without axis labels, values, thresholds, ids, or counts.
- Reading the entry reveals it once; reopening and loading the campaign retain
  the correspondence without duplicate testament or Chronicle history.
- The presentation adds no reward and does not change market ownership,
  faction relations, reputation, contracts, or moral state.
- Invalid or unresolved history remains absent rather than producing guessed
  correspondence.

## Context

This is the deferred manual smoke for the shipped capstone. Headless coverage
already exercises sealing, evidence selection, reconstruction, and replay
safety.
