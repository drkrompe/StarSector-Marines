# Tier force-ratio acceptance

Status: READY

Written: 2026-08-23

Read `mission-tier-nouns.md` before accepting this story.

## Goal

Use the tier-oriented debug surface and a deliberately chosen company to
measure whether each authored scale asks for the intended commitment.

## Acceptance

- Exercise nominal and high-risk examples at the first, middle, and top tiers
  with an appropriately sized fielded company.
- Record whether map size, lift demand, defender pressure, and optional heavy
  support read as one operation rather than separate difficulty knobs.
- Preserve the distinction between tier baseline and risk variance when tuning
  any measured value.
- Confirm a tier floor prevents invalid mission-type/scale combinations.

## Out of scope

Changing production offer policy, reward authority, or compatibility bridges.
