# Revisit legacy debug missions

Status: PROPOSED

Written: 2026-09-01

## Goal

Treat the former all-types-by-all-tiers DEBUG board as a catalog of unaccepted
ideas rather than as one implied mission roster. Revisit each useful idea on
its own terms and return only deliberate playtest entries.

## Acceptance

- Inventory the hidden debug grid, civilian-rescue stress entries, and faction
  comparison entries by the question each is meant to answer.
- For every candidate, decide whether it is a campaign mission idea, a focused
  balance fixture, a construction fixture, or obsolete scaffolding.
- A campaign-facing candidate states its progression role, objective, committed
  force, field-presence policy, opposition model, reward, and offer eligibility
  before returning to a player-visible list.
- A focused evidence candidate gets a named entry point instead of relying on a
  large synthetic client board for discoverability.
- Remove obsolete entries only after confirming no canonical fixture, evidence
  task, or regression test still depends on their factory.

## Out of scope

Re-enabling the synthetic all-missions grid as a permanent player-facing client.
