Model: `colony-breach-nouns.md` (Origin: it recurs).

## Scope

Make outbreaks a recurring sector phenomenon in the campaign, extending the
Civilian Rescue campaign event (`CivilianRescueSpawnSystem`,
`CivilianRescueEvent`) rather than replacing it:

- which colonies can wake an outbreak (population, industry such as mining,
  age of settlement), at what cadence, and how word reaches the player (intel,
  news, a relief call);
- what an unanswered outbreak does to the colony (stability, population,
  output) and what a rescue, partial rescue or collapsed mother bore changes;
- outbreak flavour by market: the colony type picks the outlying installation
  in the precinct plan;
- save-safe state and evidence per the campaign conventions.

To be broken into concrete slices when the spine and lore guide have landed.
