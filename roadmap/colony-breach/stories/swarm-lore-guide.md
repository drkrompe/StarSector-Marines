# Swarm lore guide

Status: READY

Written: 2026-10-21

Model: `colony-breach-nouns.md` (Origin). Conventions: `faction-lore-nouns.md`
and the existing `*-lore-guide.md` docs.

## Scope

Write `roadmap/factions/design/swarm-lore-guide.md` in the shape of the other
lore guides, every claim tagged with its evidence tier (this is almost all
**mod choice**, checked against the installed game so it contradicts no canon):

- what the weapon is (a self-perpetuating late-Domain / AI War-era biological
  denial weapon), what it is not (a species, a faction, a society);
- the life cycle as the strains and bores express it: dormancy, waking under a
  dense colony, eruption, harvest, growth, surges, the mother bore;
- why it recurs across the sector and what ending an outbreak does and does not
  mean;
- what in-world people call it (a few names by who says them: colonists,
  militia, Hegemony, Tri-Tachyon, the Church) and a recommended working name for
  UI text, offered to the owner as a choice, not settled;
- the open question of its maker, with the speculation each faction would
  voice and the rule that no text settles it;
- tone and vocabulary guidance for briefings, radio lines and item text
  (feeding `breach-audio.md` and mission copy).

Register the guide in `faction-lore-nouns.md`'s catalog. No code.

## Acceptance

- The owner has a short list of name candidates to pick from.
- Every claim is tiered; nothing is presented as Starsector canon.
