# Station theme fills

Status: PROPOSED

Written: 2026-08-23

Read `mapgen-nouns.md` before implementing this story.

Fill carved station room purposes with authored command, habitation, hangar,
and service-space identities. A theme is complete only when its fixtures,
movement, cover, and sightlines reinforce its tactical job rather than merely
changing decoration.

## Acceptance

- Each shipped room theme is selected from explicit semantic purpose.
- Decorative placements cannot silently alter topology; structural fixtures
  declare their tactical effect.
- The complete layout remains connected and deployable across representative
  deterministic seeds.
