# City structure injection

Status: PARKED

Written: 2026-08-23

Read `mapgen-nouns.md` before reviving this story.

Do not add generic structures for variety. When a concrete city objective or
tactical need exists, introduce one structure through the recipe with explicit
purpose, ingress, and deployment constraints.

## Re-entry condition

A named game need identifies the structure's tactical job and the city family
that should own it.

## Acceptance when resumed

- The structure has authored circulation and room/compound semantics.
- Connectivity, objectives, and defender deployment remain valid.
- It does not become a broad generator conditional or a decorative topology
  mutation.
