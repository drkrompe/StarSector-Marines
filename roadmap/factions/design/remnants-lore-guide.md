## Canon anchors

Remnants are autonomous AI war fleets developed by Tri-Tachyon and left in quarantined
regions after the AI Wars. Their surviving infrastructure includes autonomous stations
that combine supply depot, robotic factory, strategic command nexus, and weapon
platform. Remnant ships are purpose-built around automation, advanced energy and
kinetic weapons, shields, coordinated maneuver, and the absence of human life-support
requirements.

Current doctrine gives them high officer quality, high aggression, no retreat,
coordinated maneuver, strong flux systems, and broad access to advanced weapons and
hull systems. Their ranks and posts are blank human-facing abstractions, and their
fleets are named fragments, sub-ordos, ordos, and nexuses. Canon preserves uncertainty
about individual cores, command relationships, motives, degradation, and what remains
under anyone's control.

## Strong ground-war inferences

- A Remnant surface force is a machine ecology of sensors, drones, fabrication,
  command nodes, power, repair, and weapons—not human infantry in cyan armor.
- Units can be locally specialized and expendable while the network protects scarce
  cores, fabrication capability, archives, and strategic objectives.
- Coordination can be excellent without omniscience. Sensors, communication, terrain,
  damage, and compartmentalized command still constrain what a unit knows.
- Dormancy, partial isolation, corrupted orders, experimental sub-networks, and
  differing core personalities create encounter variety without making machines act
  randomly.
- A captured node can reveal, redirect, isolate, or awaken systems; it should not
  automatically grant effortless ownership of an intelligent network.

## Marine Operations direction

### Units and protection

Use disposable scouts, sensor motes, security drones, mobile weapon platforms,
repair/fabrication units, heavy breach machines, remote turrets, and command-linked
specialists. If humanoid forms appear, give them a functional reason such as using
human-scale corridors or tools. Protection should derive from chassis role, armor,
shields if explicitly implemented, redundancy, and repair—not a faction-wide damage
exception.

AI cores belong in command, facility, or rare mobile roles. Do not use an alpha core
as generic loot inside every elite drone.

### Weapons and equipment

Prefer integrated energy weapons, precise kinetics, guided systems, EMP, advanced
sensors, point defense, and purpose-built breaching or denial tools. Human small arms
may be mounted when existing infrastructure demands them, but should look adapted to
a platform rather than issued to a “robot marine.”

Remnant equipment is usually not a player-ready template. Salvage may yield components,
data, damaged weapons, or a dangerous core custody problem before it yields a usable
infantry item.

### Tactics and command

Favor rapid information sharing, coordinated concentration, sacrificial probes,
machine-speed response, layered sensor coverage, and relentless defense of strategic
nodes. Preserve honest fog of war: jamming, occlusion, severed relays, false contacts,
and local autonomy should matter. A Remnant should feel alien through priorities and
coordination, not through cheating.

### Facilities and objectives

Good sites include buried nexuses, drone factories, repair cells, sensor lattices,
power and cooling plants, data vaults, automated magazines, dormant launch bays,
quarantine installations, and human facilities colonized by machines. Geometry may
omit human comfort while retaining maintenance access and inherited Domain interfaces.

Capturing power segmentation, sensor relays, fabrication control, quarantine locks,
or a subordinate command node can make orbital engagement survivable or isolate the
primary intelligence. Destroying the core site may be safer than capture, but a patron
seeking research, control codes, or an AI core creates a credible preservation mandate.

### Missions and voice

Remnants are usually targets, hazards, or hidden actors rather than ordinary public
clients. Credible operations include survey, quarantine repair, data extraction,
core capture, survivor rescue, destruction of a nexus, disabling orbital batteries,
recovering Tri-Tachyon control material, or escaping an awakened facility. Any direct
communication should be specific to the encountered intelligence and information
available; avoid one generic robot voice.

## Anti-caricature rules

- Do not write Remnants as a single hive mind with universal knowledge.
- Do not make them emotionless humans or comedy robots by default; preserve uncertainty
  about cognition and motive.
- Do not grant mechanical immunity merely because a unit is automated.
- Do not make every Remnant operation an extermination mission; containment, theft,
  study, communication, and accidental awakening all fit the setting.
