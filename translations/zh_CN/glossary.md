# Simplified Chinese glossary

Shared terms for `translations/zh_CN`, so every slice says the same thing the
same way. Proper names (characters, ships, hull classes, factions, places) stay
in Latin script as the story docs spell them: Castle, Morgan Hale, Nessa Venn,
Zoey Venn, Second Wind, Cerberus, Hermes, Nebula, Claim, Jangala, Corvus,
Greywater Down, Wayline, Hegemony, Morrow, Tamsin Vale, Elian Dormer,
Iona Serr, Lenna, Agreus, Arcadia, Ko Combine, North Weir, Low Quay, Latchkey,
Black Sluice, Greyhook, Sable, Asharu, Junction, Cinder Steps. Loadout card codes
(ARM-HEG-3-PWR (QS)) stay as printed. Mission titles are translated (below). Whether to switch factions to the community's
established names (Hegemony as 霸主) is a translation-review question.

## Open for translation review

Slices written in parallel settled some words differently. Pick one and sweep:

- hardpoint: 硬点 (Mech Lab) vs 挂点 (shuttles, battle doll)
- plating: 装甲板 (Mech Lab, battle doll) vs 装甲 (shuttle fittings)
- scrap: 拆解 (Mech Lab) vs 报废 (shuttles)
- tier: 等级 (Barracks) vs 级 (refit)
- holders (Black Sluice): 守备队 (board, briefing, debrief) vs 守卫 (stationing)
- estate guards: 庄园卫兵 (briefing, objectives) vs 庄园守卫 (stationing)
- the heart: 心脏 (arc radio) vs 母体之心 (chapter flavour, Heart HUD)
- Hegemony and other factions: Latin script now; the community uses 霸主 and others
